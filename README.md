# writing

A multi-book writing workspace — three books, one shared writers' room.

| Book | What it is | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|
| **[books/spytwins](books/spytwins)** | Middle-grade mystery series — twins solving cases while their parents are secretly spies. 40 books planned. | Book 1 complete and edited |
| **[books/mybyb](books/mybyb)** | Gift-book survival guide for your first decade at work | Reboot drafting |
| **[books/youngnick](books/youngnick)** | *Young Nicholas* — the secret origin of Santa Claus | Bible drafted |

## Where things are

- **[books/](books/)** — one directory per book, each with its full history
- **[studio/](studio/)** — shared style, pipeline, and pitch packaging
- **[.claude/agents/](.claude/agents/)** — nine editorial specialists, usable on
  any book
- **[CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md)** — how the workspace works and the rules that hold
  across all three books

Each book's own README (and `CLAUDE.md`, where it has one) is the entry point
for that project.

## History

These books previously lived in separate repos — `MYBYB`, `Spytwins`, and
`Youngnick`. Their commits were merged here in full and those repos are
archived; this workspace is now the single home for the work.
