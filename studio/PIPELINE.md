# Pipeline

The stages a book moves through here, and what has to be true to leave each
one. A book's README should say which stage it's in.

## 1. Premise

A logline, the hook, and why this book and not another one.

*Leaves when:* the logline survives being said out loud to someone who hasn't
read anything.

## 2. Bible

Characters, world rules, themes, and the shape of the plot. Open questions are
listed explicitly rather than left implied.

*Leaves when:* the questions that would force a rewrite are locked. Not all of
them — the ones that change structure.

## 3. Outline

Scene list. Each entry names the point-of-view character, what they want, what
blocks them, and what changes by the end of the scene.

*Leaves when:* no scene exists only to move information.

## 4. Draft

Write it through. Resist revising backward — leave `[TK ...]` and keep going.

*Leaves when:* there is an ending. A bad ending still counts.

## 5. Revision

The four passes in [STYLE.md](STYLE.md), run separately and in order. Continuity
comes last because earlier passes invalidate it.

*Leaves when:* no `[TK ...]` markers remain and the continuity pass is clean.

## 6. Out the door

Query letter, synopsis, comps, submission list. Materials live in `studio/`,
since the work of pitching generalizes across books.
