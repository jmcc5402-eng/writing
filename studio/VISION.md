# The Vision (the author's steering wheel)

_This page belongs to the author. The showrunner re-reads it every
night before doing anything; changing a sentence here reorients the
whole studio by morning. Everything in it is direction, not canon —
canon lives in each book's own docs._

## The model

Agents drive the momentum; the author gives the vision and steers.
Anything requiring the author is a PR. The open-PR queue is the
author's to-do list; merges are decisions; nothing waits on memory.

## Standing direction (author, as of 2026-08-05)

- **Spytwins:** Book 1 is done for now (~95%); final polish batched
  once Books 1–3 exist; PUBLISH when four books are written. Book 2
  is rung-3 frozen — briefs next; prose only on the author's "draft."
- **Young Nicholas:** the old material needs creative pressure, not
  reverence — instruments over nostalgia. Lock Book 1's open
  decisions, then the four-book shape.
- **MYBYB:** concept locked, reboot drafting — the slow burner;
  keep it warm, don't force it.
- **Craft:** programmatic drafting (the rung contract) is the house
  method. Detail density varies by design. The series floats —
  cross-book chronology never blocks.

## What "good" looks like each morning

Every book moved one visible increment overnight, or the nudge names
the exact gate it waits at. The nudge ends with THE ONE THING (under
15 minutes) and asks at most ONE steering question.
