# The author's notes — the ledger

**Author directive, 2026-09-04:** *"I want to set up a system where my
comments are regularly reviewed by the agents as another mechanism
to avoid pitfalls that I don't like."* This file is the raw half of
that system: every comment the author makes, verbatim, in one place.
The distilled half is `studio/AUTHOR-TASTE.md`, which every drafter
and panel reads. How the two files are fed and reviewed is law in
`studio/PIPELINE.md`, "The author's notes."

## How this file works

- **Append-only, dated, verbatim.** Typos and all. A paraphrase is
  marked PARA. Never edit a row's words after the fact; add a row.
- **Sources:** PR comments (pulled at every fold from the merged PR),
  audio-listen notes (the day they are transcribed), chat rulings
  (the same day, by whoever heard them — "decisions leave the thread
  fast"). A comment that exists only in a conversation does not
  exist to any agent.
- **Every row carries a status:** RAW (recorded, not yet acted on) ·
  LAW (name the doc and section it became) · BAN (RECENT.md) ·
  DECISION (the D-number) · APPLIED (a one-off edit, done) ·
  OBSERVATION (not a directive; recorded for pattern-finding).
- **A repeat is a finding.** When the author says a thing that is
  already LAW, the check that was supposed to catch it failed; log
  the repeat here and open a BACKLOG item against the check.
- **Claude-authored PR comments are not the author's**, even though
  they post under the author's account. They carry the "Generated by
  Claude Code" footer. Only footer-less comments and quoted author
  speech are rows.
