# 15 — Cast stakes (write with the characters; audit at the outline)

Every named character has something to lose, the reader knows it in
the moment they are on the page, and some of it is dark. The spec is
`studio/craft/stakes.md`. This becomes `books/<book>/canon/STAKES.md`.

## The sheet

| Who | The stake (what they stand to lose) | Self-inflicted? | The lever (which thing the story turns on could this touch?) | How it drops (through whose eye; a line, a smell, a bill) | Resolved? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

## The audit questions, per character

1. What do they stand to lose, in one sentence a reader would repeat?
2. Did they do this to themselves? (Prefer yes.)
3. STAKE × LEVER: which thing the plot already turns on could this
   stake touch? If nothing — is it worth a line, or is it texture?
4. How will the reader learn it in a close-third book — through the
   POV lead's senses or the town's knowledge? Name the first drop.
5. Is the vice real and costed, or a costume?
6. Is the author owed a resolution? (Usually no. Say which.)

## Locked vs proposed

A stake is PROPOSED until the author says. Locked stakes get a row in
`canon/FACTS.md` (an amount, a "never shown") so the page cannot
drift them. The brief's STAKES ON THE PAGE names which of these a
chapter carries; the panel's STANCE test checks the reader got it.
