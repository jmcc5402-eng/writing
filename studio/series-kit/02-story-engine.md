## 2. Story structure

- **Snowflake:** one sentence → one paragraph (setup, 2–3 escalating
  disasters, resolution) → four paragraphs (act by act) → chapters.
- **The beat spine:** <8–12 numbered beats every book hits, in order.
  Adapt from the PreSnowflake shape: problem → obstacles → clues →
  herrings → point of no return → major disaster → resolution.>
- **Timing overlay:** `studio/craft/hauge.md` — five turning points at
  ~10/25/50/75/90%, applied to the OUTER story.
- **The millimeter rule (series mode):** inner change completes across
  the SERIES, not within a book. Editors flag inner change that
  arrives too fast, not too slow.

## Warnings, not walls

Two tiers, and auditors must report them differently.

**Walls (block, always):** locked canon facts; the audience band; fair
play (no luck, no coincidence); the heroes earn the win; book-specific
safety rails; the author's voice.

**Warnings (default, waivable):** every numeric or cadence rule in
this file. Waive per book with one recorded line in that book's
premise doc:

    WAIVED: <rule> — <reason> (author, <date>)

A violation WITH a waiver reports as compliant-by-waiver; without one
it is a finding. Bending is fine; bending must be on purpose and on
the record.
