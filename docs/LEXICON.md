# MYBYB Lexicon

The proprietary vocabulary of MYBYB. This is the reusable core of the brand — the terms travel across every format (book, deck, seminar, gift book, cards, podcast). Keep them consistent everywhere.

## Core terms

| Term | Expansion | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **MYBYB** | Make Your Boss Your Bitch | The philosophy/lifestyle: take responsibility for your work and ensure others do the same. Also used as a verb ("you'll MYBYB") and an outcome. |
| **Killer** | — | What you want to be: dependable, trusted by higher ups, can gather a team for big projects, always learning. |
| **CFC** | Crystal Fu\*king Clear | Ensuring a concept is (1) communicated and (2) communicated well. The antidote to lack of clarity. |
| **BLUF** | Bottom Line Up Front | State the point first, then support it. Applied iteratively: book → chapter → paragraph. |
| **BIU** | Bump It Up | Aligning the current task/question with the larger strategy or project. |
| **NR** | Non-Reality | The affliction of bosses who think every thought in their head was assigned, and who "think big" while ignoring feasibility. |

## Roles

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Boss** | The person with the closest authority to you. |
| **Higher Ups** | Your boss's boss (or higher), and sometimes your boss's colleagues. |
| **Colleagues** | Other people, generally at your level. |
| **Micromanager** | Boss who insists on hearing every detail; slows communication. |
| **Microfollower** | Employee who feels they must report every detail; also breaks communication. *(Def. referenced but not yet written — see Backlog.)* |

## Signature phrases / one-liners

These are quotable, merch-able, and social-ready. Treat them as assets.

- "Bosses need to be **helpful, not hopeful.**"
- "You rise to your highest level of incompetence." *(the Peter Principle — attributed idea, not original)*
- "**Improvement over perfection.**"
- "To change, you must do things **differently.**"
- "Mission / Vision / Values = **Crap / Crap / Crap**"
- "A land of **unicorns and butterflies**" (where NR bosses live)
- "Things appear to make problems simple, but if not used correctly, they make life harder."
- The **80/20 rule of intangibles**: "Difficult intangible problems usually consist of 80% tangible parts."

## Motivation model (loose idea, from a 2017 margin note)

**Impact vs. Ego vs. Ideology** — three things that motivate people at work:
- **Impact people** — unhappy unless real good actually happens.
- **Ego people** — focused on themselves ("I can afford a Beamer, life is good") even if not serving the customer.
- **Ideology people** — doing things for outside ideas, not always aligned with the customer.
