# MYBYB — Positioning & Roadmap

## What it is

An edgy, short, funny workplace-survival book. Half satire, half genuinely useful management/self-leadership advice. Written for the person stuck under an incompetent boss who wants to *do something about it* — improve their situation, their team's, and (if the boss won't get on board) route around them. Read on a phone, at lunch, or "at night with a beer."

**One-liner:** *Make Your Boss Your Bitch — ten skills to stop suffering under incompetence and start running the room.*

## Voice

Irreverent, profane-when-it-lands, confident, self-deprecating ("does the whole book suck (like this one)?"). Real frameworks under the jokes. Think *manager who's seen it all, telling you the truth over a drink.*

## Comps & the "I was first" note

- **The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F\*ck** (Manson, **2016**). MYBYB's core drafts date to **2014** — the profane-self-help-title lane was one you were writing in *before* that book broke it open. Worth stating in the pitch: not a follow-on, a parallel origin.
- **Go the F\* to Sleep** (Mansbach, 2011). The "**edgy fake-format**" lane — an adult book wearing a wholesome costume. This is the reference for the *illustrated* MYBYB spin-off, **not** an actual children's book.
- Adjacent shelf: *Getting to Yes*, *First, Break All the Rules*, *Who Moved My Cheese?* — all referenced inside the manuscript itself.

## Audience

The 40-hour-a-week employee. The lexicon and scenarios (meetings, status reports, commutes, email overload, the "stagecoach" team) are built around **traditional 9-to-5 / 40-hour work-week culture** — that's the native habitat and the target reader.

## The agile, multi-platform ladder

Fiction ladder (picture book → graphic novel → novel) is **parked** — MYBYB is nonfiction satire. The nonfiction ladder:

1. **Ship first — the short book.** Finish/lock the ~10–15k manuscript. Cheapest path to "something out the door." Self-pub / Kindle fits the "short & cheap vs. $24 fluff" argument the book itself makes.
2. **Illustrated edgy gift book.** The *Go the F\* to Sleep* move — the frameworks (Stagecoach, Bell Curve, Olympic Rings) as spreads. Giftable, shareable, visual brand identity.
3. **Deck / seminar / course.** Already teased in-book as "the MYBYB seminar."
4. **Card deck / field guide.** The lexicon and frameworks as a physical toy/tool.
5. **Podcast / brand.** The voice is podcast-native.
6. **📺 Parked: TV show.** Satirical office universe built from the boss archetypes (the Hoper, the Micromanager, the NR visionary). Long-horizon idea, not now.

**The through-line asset:** the **Lexicon** (`docs/LEXICON.md`) + the named **frameworks**. Build those once; every format is a rendering of them.
