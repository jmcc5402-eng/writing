# New Book Outline

Guide the author from a premise to a chapter outline using the project's story engine. Keep the author's concept and voice central — you organize the plot; you do not replace their vision.

## Steps

1. **Load the engine.** Read the project's series bible / story-engine, concept, and characters docs if they exist (check CLAUDE.md for pointers). If the project has an outlining worksheet, use it. Note the "must-have" checklist and the tone.
2. **Gather inputs** (ask for anything missing): setting, target reading age, desired length (chapters / word count), the core mystery or conflict, any new skill / gadget / lesson this installment introduces, and any long-arc seeds to plant.
3. **Run the Snowflake:**
   - One sentence capturing the whole book.
   - One paragraph: setup, 2–3 escalating disasters, resolution.
   - Expand to ~4 paragraphs (act by act).
   - Break into a chapter-by-chapter beat outline.
4. **Structure check:** list every planted clue and where it pays off; confirm there are no dangling setups, no luck-based solutions, that stakes escalate, and that the climax lets the lead use their established strength. Confirm every "must-have" checklist item is placed.
5. **Save** the outline into the project's `plots/` folder (or wherever outlines live) and summarize what to decide next.

For heavy plotting or a full structural pass, hand off to the **plot-architect** agent.
