# Archive — Source Material & Project History

Catalog of the original material in the author's Google Drive folder **aSpyTwins**,
which seeded this repository. Kept here so nothing is lost and the project's
evolution is traceable. The canonical, cleaned versions now live in the main
folders; these are the raw origins.

## How the project evolved (name history)

The concept has been in development for years. Character names changed across
drafts — now **locked** in [`../concept/premise.md`](../concept/premise.md):

1. **2017–2019 drafts:** twins *Jenna & Jeremy Travers*, "Uncle James," family
   traveling **12 cities in one year** (one month each). First full book concept:
   **"Missing Turtles"** (Waikiki) — turtles vanishing from an aquarium.
2. **Early 2025 drafts:** twins *Jack & Jill*; the Hawaii mystery reworked around
   **petroglyphs and stolen artifacts**.
3. **Current canon:** twins **Amanda & Andrew Wilson** (Seattle), **3 months** per
   city, Book 1 = **"The Petroglyph Mystery"** (Maui).

## Retired / superseded ideas worth keeping

- **"Missing Turtles" (aquarium)** — a strong, kid-friendly mystery (an aquarium
  about to be bought out; a boy who frees the turtles thinking he's helping). Not
  used for Book 1, but a great candidate to rework for a later book.
- **San Francisco / Pier 39 sushi mystery** — solar streetlights miswired into a
  sushi restaurant's fridge ruin the fish overnight. A fun science-y case for a
  future book.
