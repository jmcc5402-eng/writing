## Drafting order (recommendation)

Chapters are drafted blind and stitched later, so waves may run in
parallel; order between waves is about learning cheap:

- **Wave 1 — ch11, ch8, ch12.** The load-bearing payoff chapters. If
  a manifest can't support its payoff, discover it here, where a fix
  backpropagates cheapest.
- **Wave 2 — ch2, ch6, ch9, ch10.** The mystery spine: incident,
  accusation, setback, stakeout.
- **Wave 3 — ch1, ch3, ch4, ch5, ch7.** Texture-heavy chapters;
  their gags and plants are fully specified below.

## Seam pass (note)

Transitions are deliberately left rough. No drafter writes toward a
neighboring chapter — openings and endings serve THIS chapter only.
After all twelve chapters exist, a dedicated seam pass stitches each
chapter's STATE OUT to the next chapter's STATE IN (protocol
instrument 3). Trailer-voice endings are banned anyway (see every
brief's banned moves).
