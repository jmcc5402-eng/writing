### Ch 2 — The Shutter Row

### Ch 5 — Falling Practice

roll, come up quiet — the instructor's whole point is that falling
well is a skill, not an accident, and both twins get genuinely
absorbed in getting it right. Amanda likes that it has rules; Andrew
likes that it has physics (planted small; nobody calls it anything
but a class).

At home the case gives Andrew two gifts. His macro shot of the
newest mark shows a crisp ridge along every curve — stencil edges,
not freehand; whoever paints these is being exact, reproducing
something, not tagging. And the running gag turns: Andrew, fully
converted, gives the family a deadpan spec-tour of the apartment's
toilet — "heated seat. HEATED. SEAT." (gag touch 2) — while the

### Ch 6 — Paint on His Hands

STATE IN: day D-10(Mon) / rain at dawn / Kenta raking ice early,
ahead of the six-o'clock fish truck (the one morning he beats it);
Rin has redone panel one — the mark lands on the HAMADA

Escalation #3, and it's personal: the new mark is on Kenta's own
family shutter, found by Kenta himself at first light (his dawn
ice-raking job putting him there with zero contrivance). The reader

shows a boy alone in the rain with a rag, scrubbing at his family's
name. By the time the association crowd gathers, the mark is

He goes silent instead of explaining. Not sullen — locked, because
explaining this morning means explaining the morning weeks ago that
nobody knows about, and the row is already doing its arithmetic out
