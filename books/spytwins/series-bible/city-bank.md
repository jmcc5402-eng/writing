# City Bank

_Great nuggets about cities, saved for future books. Started 2026-07-30
(author decision: cities may repeat across 40 books — don't spend a
great nugget on a book that doesn't need it, and don't lose it when the
research note gets filed away)._

## Rules

1. **Research notes feed the bank.** After a culture-researcher pass,
   anything strong that the current book does NOT use gets a row here.
2. **Status is per nugget:** `BANKED` (unused, available) or
   `SPENT (B#)` (on the page in that book — cite the chapter once
   drafted). A nugget referenced but not really used stays BANKED.
3. **Repeat-city books shop here first.** A return to a city starts by
   reading its section; the outline brief includes the city's BANKED
   rows.
4. **Source stays attached.** Each nugget points at the research note
   that verified it — the bank is an index, not a second copy of the
   facts.
5. Branding/sensitivity flags travel with the nugget — a banked nugget
   is still subject to the fictionalize-brands gate and age band.
