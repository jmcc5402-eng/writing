## The twins

### Amanda Wilson
- **Age/role:** one of the twins (the pattern-solver).
- **Loves:** soccer (a powerful left foot — Coach Chen back in Seattle called it
  the team's best), solving patterns, logic problems, and riddles for fun.
- **Wants to be:** an astronaut or a painter.
- **Tells & habits:** carries a notebook everywhere and sketches what she sees;
  **bites her lip when she's working a tricky problem or feels uncertain.**
- **Function in the mysteries:** the analyst. She spots the pattern, connects the
  clues, and builds the map.

### Andrew Wilson
- **Age/role:** the other twin (the tech/gadget guy).
- **Plays:** lacrosse — though he's still working to get good at it.
- **Loves:** technical gadgets and drones; **desperately wants a cell phone**,
  but his parents say not until high school. Sometimes likes to be alone.
- **Wants to be:** a race-car driver or a doctor.
- **Tells & habits:** plays with his hair / smooths his cowlick when nervous.
- **Function in the mysteries:** the field tech. Photographs evidence, runs the
  tablet research, pilots the drone.

**The twin dynamic:** Amanda is decisive and quick; Andrew is careful and
curious. They bicker like siblings but are fiercely loyal and complete each
other's thinking — she finds the pattern, he finds the proof.

## The parents

### Erin Wilson (Mom)
- **Public role:** management consultant; travels for client projects.
- **Secret:** an operative for a mysterious government agency _(the series' long
  reveal — see [concept](../concept/premise.md))._
- **Traits:** always on her phone/laptop; wishes she had more time to exercise
  and garden; wishes Adam grilled more fish and less meat. **Knows suspiciously
  much about things like security-camera blind spots** — a planted clue.
- **Sister:** Jenny.

### Adam Wilson (Dad)
- **Public role:** management consultant, same track as Erin.
- **Secret:** also an agency operative.
- **Traits:** loves to grill (burgers, teriyaki chicken, fresh fish); wishes he
  had more time to mountain bike; the fun, slightly indulgent parent who buys the
  kids cool gadgets — partly out of guilt for missing their games.

**The parents' pattern:** they insist on **at least one family meal a day**, but
"clients" constantly pull them into video calls that interrupt dinners and make
them miss soccer and lacrosse games. Some "meetings" pointedly can't happen over
video — a recurring seed of the secret.

## Extended family

### Aunt Jenny & Uncle Jacob
- **Jenny** is Erin's sister.
- They **video-call at the worst possible moment**, every book — a running gag.
- Always have a witty or clever thing to say to the twins.
- **Uncle Jacob** is oddly worldly (dig sites in Peru, documentation know-how) —
  his "helpful facts" often unknowingly hand the twins the key idea. Another
  quiet seed that this family is not what it seems.
- The parents often complain that Jenny and Jacob "keep getting themselves into
  trouble."

## The pets

### Blazer (dog)
- Part Labrador, part German Shepherd. **Has allergies.**

  peanut-butter-smeared note) cracks the case.

## Book-1 guest cast (Maui)

_Local to Book 1; not necessarily recurring._

- **Kekoa** — teenage neighbor; drone pilot; teaches the twins to surf. The
  twins' local guide and third member of the crew. **Book 1 is his
  summer break** — Maui schools are out, which is why his weekday
  mornings are free; the twins' online school runs its own year-round
  calendar, which is why THEY still have first period while he
  doesn't. [Locked by author PR, 2026-08-04.]
- **Mrs. Kalani** — Kekoa's grandmother; works at the cultural center; expert in
  Hawaiian petroglyphs and culture. Sharper than she lets on (she tracks the kids
  and reviews security footage to save the day).
- **Dr. Marcus Lee** — the villain; a disgraced former University of Hawaii
  archaeologist stealing artifacts to "prove" his rejected migration theory. Not
  evil so much as desperate and misguided.
- **Coach Chen** — Amanda's soccer coach back in Seattle (mentioned only).
