## Part 1 — Verification of the three known errors

All three errors flagged in the critique (Part 2, item 6) are **CONFIRMED
on the page**, with one nuance on fix direction.

### E1 — Ch2: library vs. cultural center. CONFIRMED.

The whole Ch2 outing is unambiguously at the **library**:

> "The walk to the library took them past a shave ice stand"

> "At the local library, just three blocks away, Andrew discovered an
> entire shelf dedicated to Hawaiian mythology and technology."

Mrs. Kalani appears as they leave *that* building and invites them to the
cultural center; they **decline**:

> "Thanks so much Mrs. Kalani, but I don't think we have time. We need to
> get home for lunch. Maybe we can come back tomorrow with Kekoa."

Then the very next paragraph:

> "The late morning sun blazed overhead as Andrew and Amanda took the
> hiking trail home from the cultural center."

They were never at the cultural center. Fix direction is the author's
call [TK: either change to "home from the library," or restage the scene
at the cultural center — but see E2 for why the library reading is the
one the rest of the book supports].

### E2 — Ch3: "just yesterday" fishhook. CONFIRMED (downstream of E1).

Ch3, after the theft announcement:

> "They had seen it just yesterday, gleaming under the display lights."

They could not have. In Ch2 they declined the tour and never entered the
center. Ch3 is written as their **first** visit: Mrs. Kalani greets them
"pulling two visitor badges from her drawer" and sets them up as
newcomers. Note the two errors interlock: if E1 were "fixed" by making
Ch2 a cultural-center visit where they saw the display, E2 would become
true but the Ch3 first-visit staging (badges, the declined-tour line,
"You're here early!") would break instead. Fixing E1 toward the library
means E2's sentence must go or change (e.g., they'd seen its photo — but
Andrew pulls up the website photo two paragraphs later, so avoid
duplication). [TK: author to pick the direction; the two must be fixed
together.]

### E3 — Ch4: Amanda with "her" tablet. CONFIRMED.

> "Amanda sat at a desk and fired up her tablet to examine a picture of
> it."

Canon (`characters/characters.md`): the tablet is **Andrew's** signature
prop (photos, research, video calls); Amanda's is the **notebook**. This
is the only tablet-ownership slip in ch1–4 — everywhere else the tablet
is correctly Andrew's (Ch2 "Andrew clutched his tablet," Ch3 "his tablet
propped up against a stack of archaeology books," "Andrew snapped photos
with his tablet," "Andrew's tablet started buzzing"). Note Andrew is
across the room at the window with Kekoa in this scene, so "borrowed
Andrew's tablet" is a viable fix. [TK: fix wording is the author's/
line-editor's call.]

## Part 2 — Additional findings from the ch1–4 sweep

Clean bill of health first: **no retired names anywhere** (grep for
Dr. Chen / Jenna / Jeremy / Travers / Uncle James / Jack / Jill: zero
hits). Villain is "Dr. Lee" / "Dr. Marcus Lee" throughout ch7–11; "Coach
Chen" appears exactly once (Ch2), correctly as Amanda's Seattle soccer
coach, mention-only — the two are properly distinct people. Wilson
surname, Erin/Adam, Jenny/Jacob, Blazer all match locked canon. Blazer is
described in Chapter 1 as canon requires, nearly verbatim to the bible:
"he got the Lab's love of water and the Shepherd's nose - he can smell a
treat from three houses away."

New issues found (numbered E4+, severity noted):

- **E4 (minor, Ch1→Ch2 timeline).** Ch1, arrival-day dinner: "yeah, I
  guess Andrew and I will go explore tomorrow." Ch2: "The twins had spent
  most of their first week unpacking" and "Dad had finally given them
  permission to explore the neighborhood on their own." Soft
  contradiction — Amanda announces exploring "tomorrow," then a week
  passes and permission reads as newly won.
- **E5 (minor, Ch2).** Andrew is "already plotting their route to the
  nearest electronics store where he'd spotted some cool drones in the
  window" — on their *first* solo outing, before he could have walked
  past any window. The electronics-store plan is also silently dropped
  (they go to the library).
- **E6 (minor, Ch3).** Mrs. Kalani: "from talking with your mother
  yesterday, I hear you're quite good with patterns." [CHECK: this
  mother–Kalani conversation happens nowhere on the page; plausible
  off-page but unplanted, and it is the kind of detail a revision could
  accidentally contradict.]
- **E7 (minor mechanics, Ch3).** "Amanda's eyes lit up and she muted the
  tablet," speaks privately to Andrew, then resumes talking to Jenny and
  Jacob with no unmute. Also the attribution muddle just above it: "'How's
  island life treating you? I can't believe you get Wi-Fi at the beach!'
  Uncle Jacob exclaimed" — two sentences, seemingly two speakers, one tag.
- **E8 (minor, Ch3).** "Hold the measuring tape steady" — a measuring
  tape appears from nowhere; the kids packed lunches, not survey gear.
- **E9 (minor, Ch3→series, [CHECK]).** Jenny and Jacob: "Maybe we can
  make it out there in a couple weeks after we wrap up this project in
  Indonesia." They never visit anywhere in Book 1. Dropped promise —
  intentional gag or loose thread? Note "Indonesia" is a new on-page
  fact about Jenny/Jacob, not in the bible.
- **E10 (minor plausibility, Ch4).** The center closed "for the
  investigation" in Ch3 and suffered a second theft overnight, yet the
  next day three kids hang out unsupervised in Mrs. Kalani's office at
  lunch. Passable for the age band, but a revision should acknowledge
  the closure.
- **E11 (soft timeline constraint, Ch2–4).** Ch4 is the first mention of
  online classes ("During their online classes the next day"), while Ch2
  and Ch3 are full free days morning-to-afternoon. Works only if Ch2–3
  fall on a weekend (or a stated break). Any ch1–4 revision should keep
  Ch2 = Saturday-like, Ch3 = Sunday-like, Ch4 = school day, or say why
  not.
- **E12 (minor, Ch1/Ch3/Ch4/Ch5, [CHECK]).** The spiral symbol does
  triple duty: Ch1 Kekoa says "this spiral here? It could represent the
  ocean, or maybe even eternity"; Ch3/Ch4 a spiral matches the ceremonial
  bowl; Ch5 Andrew's downloaded guide says "this spiral means 'sacred
  place'". Not strictly contradictory (there can be several spirals), but
  a revision must keep these as distinct carvings or reconcile the
  meanings.
- **E13 (minor, Ch3, [CHECK]).** Two planted clues never explicitly pay
  off: the "footsteps" in the mud ("Look at these footsteps, I think
  someone else has been studying these petroglyphs too" — presumably
  Dr. Lee's, never confirmed) and the higher vine-hidden symbols seen in
  the lightning flash (Ch4 says Andrew "remembered the new markings," but
  no scene ever examines them). "Every setup pays off" is a hard rule.
- **E14 (mechanics, ch1–4 instances of a book-wide issue).** Lowercase
  dialogue after tags: Ch1 'said, "yeah, I guess Andrew and I will go
  explore tomorrow."'; Ch3 'said, "oh yes, we were just leaving."'
  Hyphens stand in for em dashes throughout. (Critique's claim verified.)
- **E15 (prop watch, out of ch1–4 but constrains them, [CHECK]).** Ch12:
  '"We should take one last picture," Andrew said, pulling out his
  camera.' A standalone camera exists nowhere else — Andrew's photo tool
  is the tablet in ch1–11. A ch1–4 revision must NOT introduce a camera;
  the Ch12 line is the drift, not the rule.

Verified-accurate critique claims worth trusting downstream: zero red
herrings in the draft; Dr. Lee named at Ch7 midpoint; Mrs. Santos exists
only in Ch10; the Ch4 dinner blind-spot slip is the arc-clue centerpiece.
