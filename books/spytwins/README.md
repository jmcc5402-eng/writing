# Spytwins

A middle-grade adventure series about twins who travel the world with their
consultant parents and solve a mystery in every new city — never suspecting
that their parents are secretly spies.

This repository is the **single source of truth** for the Spytwins franchise:
the finalized concept, the series bible, character canon, finished manuscripts,
future plots, and the go-to-market roadmap.

---

## How we work

Agile, one increment at a time. Each "sprint" ships one small, finished thing —
a locked concept, a polished manuscript, a book outline, a market test — then we
learn and pick the next increment. Nothing has to be perfect all at once; it has
to be *done and moving forward*.

_All source material was written by the author. This repo organizes and
formalizes that material; it does not replace the author's voice._
