## Synopsis

Twins **Amanda and Andrew Wilson** move to Maui for three months while their
parents take a "consulting" assignment. Near their new house they find ancient
Hawaiian **petroglyphs** — and when artifacts start vanishing from the local
cultural center, Amanda realizes each missing item matches a carved symbol. With
their new friend **Kekoa** (and his sharp grandmother **Mrs. Kalani**), the twins
decode the petroglyphs as a map to a hidden ceremonial site, follow it through a
lava-tube system, and catch **Dr. Marcus Lee** — a disgraced archaeologist
stealing artifacts to prove a rejected theory. Their dog **Blazer** cracks the
case with his nose. Along the way they learn to surf, absorb a lot of Hawaiian
culture, and discover their parents know a little *too* much about security
systems.

## Chapter map

1. **Aloha, New Home** — arrival in Maui; meet Kekoa, Mrs. Kalani, Blazer; first
   sight of the petroglyphs.
2. **Finding Their Way** — exploring town; a second set of carvings that seem to
   point somewhere.
3. **Strange Symbols** — the first artifact (a bone fishhook) vanishes; Amanda
   spots that it matches a petroglyph.
4. **Connecting the Dots** — a second artifact gone; an old map in the office;
   Mom slips about security-camera blind spots.
5. **The Ancient Map** — surf lessons; the symbols are a *trail*, pointing to the
   mountains.
6. **Underground Clues** — into the lava tubes; modern trash and a torn note prove
   someone's been there.
7. **The Archaeologist's Trail** — they identify **Dr. Marcus Lee**; plan a
   stakeout.
8. **Close Call** — night stakeout; they overhear Lee's "tomorrow night" plan and
   get caught; grounded.
9. **Race Against Time** — grounded but investigating; Blazer finds a hidden
   sealed entrance.
10. **The Sacred Site** — they reach the lava-tube chamber and the stolen
    artifacts; Lee corners them.
11. **Mystery Solved** — Blazer stops Lee; Mrs. Kalani and police arrive; the case
    is explained.
12. **Aloha Means Goodbye Too** — celebration; farewell feast; the next
    assignment is **Japan.**
