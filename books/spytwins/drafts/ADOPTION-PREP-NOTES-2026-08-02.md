## Section 2 — Three proposed ruling PRs (1-minute reads)

### PR-3 — DECISION: the ch11 parents' arrival (ledger S01 standing [TK])

Current v2 ch11 passage, verbatim:

> "You kids did some impressive detective work,"
> said the officer with the fishhook, standing up.
> "But you should have come to us sooner."
>
> "I have been saying exactly that," said their mother's voice
> from the entrance tunnel,
> "since they were old enough to find things."
>
> Their parents stood in the tunnel mouth,
> flashlights in hand, faces doing worry and relief and pride
> all at once and in no particular order.
> Their mom's eyes went around the chamber once —
> kids, dog, police, professor, artifacts —
> and Amanda braced for the storm.

As written, the arrival is unexplained — the parents left at two for a
client meeting "until after dinner," and now they are inside a lava
tube. Unexplained reads as a wink whether intended or not (audit row
13), and it would be the book's fourth.

**Option A (recommended): mundane one-liner — Mrs. Kalani called
them; no wink spent.** Exact replacement (only the third stanza
changes; two lines added):

> Their parents stood in the tunnel mouth,
> flashlights in hand, faces doing worry and relief and pride
> all at once and in no particular order.
> "Mrs. Kalani called us from the police station," their dad said.
> "We drove straight from the client's."
> Their mom's eyes went around the chamber once —
> kids, dog, police, professor, artifacts —
> and Amanda braced for the storm.

Book winks stay at three (ch1, ch4, ch11 fieldcraft) — at cap,
compliant. Timing is honest: Mrs. Kalani was at the station when the
dot left the trails (ch11 as staged).

**Option B: leave pointed (current text).** The arrival becomes a
deliberate budgeted wink — the book's fourth, over the 2–3 cap, so it
requires a recorded waiver in the premise doc per story-engine
"Warnings, not walls":

    WAIVED: wink budget (ingredient 13) — B1 carries a fourth,
    deliberately pointed beat: the parents' unexplained ch11 arrival
    (author, 2026-08-02)

The packet manuscript currently carries Option B's text (unchanged);
merging PR-3/Option A is one paste.
