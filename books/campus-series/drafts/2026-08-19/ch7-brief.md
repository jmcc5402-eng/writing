## Open from wanting

Marisol wants to sit with the first-fall ache and not be pitied for
it. Earlene's two pies, the board's manufactured "firetraps," and
the wine night all arrive through that want — and by the end of the
night what she wants has changed shape: she has a plan, and
witnesses.

This chapter debuts the series' calling card. Once per book, never
twice. The rules, complete:

- One of the ladies relays a STEAMY secondhand story — "my
  cousin," "a friend down in Birmingham" — sensory, earthy, funny
  ("twenty minutes on her back, every electrode in her body" is
  the flavor bar). Teller: your choice among Aisha and Birdie
  [drafter's call — Marisol hosts, she doesn't tell].

  knock, a cork, a phone lighting up MOM CALLING; the interruption
  is the hook; the room groans. Different interruption every book
  — this debut's interruption is yours to invent [within the
  manifest: no new named townspeople at the door].

  demanded; deniability is the game — everyone pretends to believe
  the cousin exists.

  scenes: the scene cuts at the first piece of clothing on the
  ground, inside the story too. Subjects always adults (R1 applies
  inside stories), never identifiable real people, never affairs.
