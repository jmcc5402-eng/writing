## Register (author rulings — quote-level authority)

  reader, never smirk. Old-school country vibe, modern twist.
- **Gilmore calibration:** banter-forward; a laugh near every ache.
  The argument about the House is a courtship wearing a disagreement
  — nobody's wrong, nobody yields, both are having a wonderful time
  they will each deny.
- **Register sample = accepted ch01.** Blind rule stands.
- Cal is a Robert Redford type — weathered-golden, quiet, good
  hands, funnier than he lets on; grumpiness in behavior and
  dialogue only. Marisol is SEXY, confident, a woman who knows the

  welcome.
- **Villain rule:** Boyd is charming and never stupid; he never
  raises his voice — one warm register for everything. Sentiment
  first, drawings later. No mustache-twirling.

## Standing gates (wave-1 rejections, now pre-checks — your contract)

1. **Dialogue floor:** ≥15% quoted words (target ~25%); the linter
   reports the number. **Named-speaker share ≥80%** of dialogue
   turns.
2. **Caused beats:** Cal must CAUSE ≥3 beats — he argues back, he
   chooses to stay, he does something with the card; things happen
   because he acts, not around him.
3. **Chorus on page:** named sub-characters participate — a gala is
   a town room; the town is never scenery.
4. **Warmth + Cal appeal:** both leads visibly likable, competent,
   attractive — looks, strength, humor in frame. The dress has
   opinions; so does the room.
5. **Fun floor:** one laugh and one ache, minimum, each.
6. **Notice budget:** ≥2 physical-notice beats through Cal's eyes —
   her hands, her laugh, the way she runs a table without seeming
   to; the dress. His effort not to watch is on the page.
7. **One long glance,** held a beat too long, broken by a town
   interruption (the comic valve).
8. **Wound-tells:** Cal says numbers instead of feelings, and will
   not sit with his back to the House — at a gala, the tell adapts:
   he knows where the House is from any chair he's given. Marisol
   screenshots reflexively, even happy moments. Tells only — no
   backstory spoken.
9. **10% corny:** budgeted, not banned.
