## The law (frozen beats — each an EVENT someone causes)

1. **The leak, Monday morning.** Cover sheet and summary pages of
   the report surface in the county's feeds — **cropped so the
   signature block reads as executed**. Record the artifact
   EXACTLY and inventively coldly (this is a new crop, not the
   old screenshot); by lunch the county's paraphrase is
   FACILITIES CONDEMNS HARTWELL HOUSE.
2. **The photo, by two o'clock.** The dance photo runs beside the
   leak: *he signed it and she knew; she flipped him; the crew
   pays.* Their public joy — her first real smile at him is IN
   that photograph — recut as proof of a fix. The reader knows
   every word is false; Cal's pain is watching the truest thing
   she ever gave him get captioned as strategy.
3. **The bell at the Mule, by five.** Somebody at the Blind Mule
   rings the bell for the jobs — a round bought against the
   winter work the condemnation "saves." Cal, on an errand,
   stands UNNOTICED IN THE DOORWAY (he never enters the bar)
   watching his own name buy a round he never poured.
4. **Verna's basket.** Not all of Ashford buys it: Verna Poteat
   plants her bushel basket in the Mule's doorway and announces,
   to nobody and everybody, that she has known Cal Sutter since
   he alphabetized the church pantry, and lies don't hold up a
   porch. Her defense is the chapter's one warm light — a laugh
   lives inside it (Gilmore law), and she requires no reply.
5. **Boyd's terms, by evening.** They arrive at the shop —
   paper or a visit, volume one either way: sign the real one by
   Friday (THE reset Friday — never invent a new date), or the
   winter list writes itself. The twelve, at last, priced out
   loud.
6. **The one exchange.** The leads do not meet on the page and
   do not doubt each other for one second — no misunderstanding
   exists or is hinted. One text exchange, one text each,
   artifact-clean, proving the alliance calm inside the storm
   (they are public now; texting is legal). Keep it short enough
   to break a reader's heart with how steady it is.
7. **The arithmetic.** The unbearable math, stated flat: the
   false outrage is the pledge drive's best day ever — the lie
   is funding her fight against him — and any truth he tells now
   looks like exactly what the photo says.
