# Campus Series — Thread Map (Book 1.1)

**PROPOSED — seeded 2026-08-18.** Required by the workspace
`CLAUDE.md` hard rule: every book keeps a `THREADS.md` beside the
manuscript it maps — *"a per-chapter ledger of what each chapter
introduces, carries, pays off, and hands forward, plus a thread
index."* Threads get greppable IDs (`T##` book-local, `S##`
series-level). **`OWED:` marks a plant awaiting its payoff.**

**Why this file exists now, and late.** It didn't exist while
chapters 2, 3 and 4 were drafted — nine candidates written with no
ledger of what chapter one owed. The 2026-08-18 sweep
(`notes/ch234-sweep-2026-08-18.md`) found five of chapter one's six
new plants unrecorded and four already going unused one chapter
later, and traced it here. `CLAUDE.md` also specifies that *"drafting
agents get the relevant index entries in their brief"* — so the
practical fix is that **every future chapter brief carries the OWED
rows that touch it.**

**Maintenance rule (workspace):** thread maps update when a chapter is
**accepted**, not merely drafted.

insertion (every chapter gained 5-9 lines above line 1 of prose). All
138 `chNN:LINE` citations in this file were shifted and spot-verified
against the text they name. Dated files under `notes/` and `drafts/`
were left alone — read them against `notes/line-drift-2026-09-02.md`.
