# Campus Series — Thread Map (Book 1.1)

**PROPOSED — seeded 2026-08-18.** Required by the workspace
`CLAUDE.md` hard rule: every book keeps a `THREADS.md` beside the
manuscript it maps — *"a per-chapter ledger of what each chapter
introduces, carries, pays off, and hands forward, plus a thread
index."* Threads get greppable IDs (`T##` book-local, `S##`
series-level). **`OWED:` marks a plant awaiting its payoff.**

**Why this file exists now, and late.** It didn't exist while
chapters 2, 3 and 4 were drafted — nine candidates written with no
ledger of what chapter one owed. The 2026-08-18 sweep
(`notes/ch234-sweep-2026-08-18.md`) found five of chapter one's six
new plants unrecorded and four already going unused one chapter
later, and traced it here. `CLAUDE.md` also specifies that *"drafting
agents get the relevant index entries in their brief"* — so the
practical fix is that **every future chapter brief carries the OWED
rows that touch it.**

**Maintenance rule (workspace):** thread maps update when a chapter is
**accepted**, not merely drafted.

### T01 — the wrench. Two debts, one unpaid.

1. **The object.** Cal takes it at ch 1; rev 2.2 puts it on his bench
   at ch 4, "two weeks unreturned." *(State chain note: ch 1 is Sat
   Wk2 and ch 4 is Mon Wk3 — **nine days**, not two weeks. The outline
   says two weeks too, so this is inherited. Fix in the outline or
   accept the round number.)*
2. **`OWED:` the spoken promise.** Ch 1: *"**I'll bring you the right
   one**," he said.* All three ch 4 candidates frame the object as
   returning **hers**, never bringing the **right** one — faithful to
   an outline that only says "return." **The hole is at the outline
   rung.** Cheapest fix: one clause in the winning ch 4 — Cal's eye
   going to a right-size wrench he hasn't taken down yet. That
   converts an unpaid promise into a planted one.
3. **`[TK]` its pre-book home.** `ch3-candidate-C` pins it to the
   glove box; the ch 1 reader panel's promoted graft wants C's
   *toolbox* ending. **You cannot take both.** Ratify one before

### T06 — Marisol's tell. The condition failed.

*"Smiling at a point somewhere past his ear"* (ch01:191). This row
previously read: **"Carry it in the ch 3 winner or the tell dies in
chapter one."**

It was not carried. Counting both ch 3 fields and the ch 2 and ch 4
fields, **nine blind drafters across three competitions have had this
plant available and none has used it.** Chapters 1–4 are accepted and
locked; the tell exists exactly once, in the chapter that invented it.

That is not nine drafters being careless. A tell is a *habit*, and a
habit needs a second instance to become one — but nothing in any brief
asked for the second instance, because this ledger did not exist until
after chapters 2–4 were drafted, and when it did exist it stated the
condition here rather than in the briefs where drafters read.

**The author's ruling, two ways:**

1. **Revive it** — assign a chapter and put the row in that chapter's
   brief, not only here. Ch 5 and ch 8 are the natural slots: both are
   Marisol POV with someone to deflect from, and both are still
   unwritten. A tell that returns at ch 5 and again near the midpoint
   reads as character; one that returns once at ch 20 reads as an
   author remembering.
2. **Retire it** — strike the row and let ch01:191 stand as a good
   line rather than a promise. Nothing breaks. No later chapter
   depends on it and no reader is owed it.

**Recommendation: revive at ch 5, or retire it tonight.** What should
not happen is a third field drafting past it — that is how a plant
becomes a reproach instead of a thread.
