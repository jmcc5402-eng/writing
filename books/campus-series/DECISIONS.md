**Status: PROPOSED (recorded on merge). 2026-08-07.**

Two parallel author threads built two architectures for the same
romance program:

- `studio/ideas/romance-city-quartets.md` (2026-07-30): container =

city-quartet engine onto the four-campus container:

   Maggie-to-London move in the adult grid is already this rule in

   letters mystery are one identity, budget exactly one touch per
   book, never load-bearing, mega-finale adds an HEA. [TK: decide

   (HEA, consent, locked heat band = walls; cameo counts, gag

5. **Divergences flagged, not silently resolved:** lead-age anchor

   Hartwell trust mega-arc vs. the matchmaker mega-finale ([TK:
   whether the sunset-hearing finale and the convergence-wedding

(cost: two half-architectures for one program, drifting apart);
