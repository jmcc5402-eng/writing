# Beat map — Book 1.2

Which chapter carries which beat, for every curve in
`studio/craft/curves.md`. `story-matrix.py` compares the declared
chapter against the curve's target position and reports the gap.

**Planned chapters: 30.** Written and accepted: 1–19.

Declaring a beat here is not a promise that it lands well. It is a
promise about *where*, so a slide is visible the week it happens
instead of at the retrospective.

Tolerance is ±7% of the book — about two chapters either side at this
length. A gap inside tolerance is not reported.
