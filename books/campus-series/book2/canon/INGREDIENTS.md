## Ingredient 22 — the dog, and why it is recorded rather than fixed

Book 1.2 reached chapter 19 with no dog. The only two mentions are an
offstage lost one: *"Somebody had called in about a dog"* (ch 2) and
*"a dog somebody was still missing out past the fairgrounds"* (ch 5).

**Author's ruling, 2026-09-16: too late for this book. Carry it
forward.** That is the right call — a dog bolted into the back third to
satisfy a checklist is exactly the retrofit smell this studio spent a
week learning to avoid.

What it is recorded for:

1. **1.3 and 1.4 get one at the outline gate**, not as a memory. The
   engine check will report ingredient 22 UNCLAIMED until a chapter is
   named for it.
2. **The lost dog is available.** Two chapters already mention somebody
   searching past the fairgrounds. If a later book wants it, that dog
   coming home is planted and free — but that is a 1.3 decision, not a
   patch on this one.
3. **The failure is the interesting part.** Nobody decided to drop the
   dog. It was author-stated five weeks before drafting began and the
   book simply drifted off it, the same way the superfan went quiet for
   fifteen chapters. An ingredient list that nothing checks is a list
   of good intentions.
