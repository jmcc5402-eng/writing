# Signal registers — Book 1.2

The small recurring things whose *variation* carries the feeling.
Spec: `studio/craft/registers.md`. Checked by
`studio/tools/register-check.py`.

**Format.** One block per register. `default` is the ordinary state and
must be boring. Each spend names the chapter, the substitution, and
what it says — and the substitution must appear in that chapter and
nowhere else.

---

## Dan — the drink

- **default:** `beer`
- **established by:** ch 6

| Ch | Substitution | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | `wine !wine night` | in a house, not a room — the best week he has had |
| 26 | `bourbon\|whiskey` | the dark night *(planned, not yet written)* |

> **OPEN — the bourbon may already be spent.** `ch04:386` gives Dan a
> whiskey, two fingers, unasked, the night of the injury: *"and set a
> glass in front of Dan. Whiskey, two fingers. No invitation, no
> bill."* That is at 13% of the book and it is the same signal the
> ch-26 spend is reserving. Three ways out, author's call:
> 1. **Change ch 4** to beer, and keep bourbon clean for the dark night.
> 2. **Keep both and make it a rhyme** — the ch-26 page notices it is
>    the second glass anyone has set in front of him without asking,
>    which is stronger than either alone but must be written on purpose.
> 3. **Move the dark-night spend** to something else entirely.
>
> Doing nothing is the only bad option: the reader meets bourbon as
> ordinary at ch 4 and it cannot carry weight at ch 26.

*(ch 3's bourbon is Verna offering it to Aisha, not Dan drinking — a
different character and an offer, not a spend. Texture, not signal.)*

## Dan — the seat *(not mechanically checkable — panel's eye)*

- **default:** the stool nearest the register, with the register
  between it and hers
- **established by:** ch 5, seeded again at ch 9 and ch 12

The spend at ch 19 is *somebody else on her stool* — thirty-four with a
taped hand. There is no term to grep for: the signal is an absence and
a substitution of person, not of noun. Declared here so a drafter knows
it exists and does not spend it twice; verified by the panel, not by
`register-check.py`.

**This is the honest limit of the tool.** A register carried by a noun
(a drink, a coat) is checkable. A register carried by a situation is
not.

## Ratchet — the tail *(Book 1.1; series furniture)*

- **default:** `two thumps` for crew trucks, one for a delivery van
- **established by:** 1.1 ch 2

A graded code the reader learns early and reads faster than dialogue.
**Spending it wrong is a canon error, not a style choice** — 1.1 ch 14
gave Cal's most exposed line a single thump, which under the code reads
as the delivery-van grade. Caught by the continuity sweep, 2026-09-15.

## Not yet running here, worth considering for 1.3

- **Sound.** Cal ran the campus by ear in 1.1 and it was one of the
  book's best textures. Nothing in 1.2 uses hearing as a register.
- **Food.** The Coach's Slice is furniture and has never varied.
- **Vehicle.** The two-door truck does real work in the leak, but as a
  plot object rather than a register — it never changes to say
  something.
