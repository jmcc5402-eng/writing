## THE OPENING, SAID

The first paragraph says what today is and what is at stake: a
Saturday with no game, the annex empty, and her in the records room
with boxes she went and got herself because Facilities' mailbox was
full — packing thirty years of paper that will be evidence when

the town on the board saying she talks down to families.

## THE ARGUMENT, SAID

She wants one place that will keep her; the building she unpacked
is being taken and the town she stood up for turned on her by
midnight; so she does the one thing she knows, which is to make the
record perfect and ask nobody for anything. Then a hundred-year-old
letter tells her what staying was for, and she pockets it instead
of answering it.
