What this chapter does to the feeling: it gives them the best week

## THE LEADS' SCENES (the floor)

The leads share MOST of the chapter. The floor: **New Year's Eve at
his house, no fewer than 1,500 words** — her face and his at every
turn; at least two awkward beats (this is his house and she has
never seen it; he cleaned; she notices); the approach to the first
touch at least four sentences; the talk before the cut is the
chapter's longest talk and is about THEM (what the week has been;
what Friday is; nothing about her calls); the cut at the first
garment; the night told in a clause. **The MONTAGE** (Sunday to
Wednesday, nights and days): a run of paragraphs, each ONE picture
with ONE line of talk in it — never a list, never "they did X and Y
and Z." Each paragraph ends; no paragraph is one sentence. **The
last section THINKS** — Friday, the plates coming back, what he
fears (the county; February; the woman across a sheet again on
Monday) — and COMES BACK to her before the end. **APART, WHY:** one
plain sentence, early, says why the week is secret (the terms:
nothing public till the first Wednesday in February; his truck is
never in that lot; the county is home Friday). No volleys anywhere.

**WOVEN:** she is in every scene of his, in person or in his head —
and his head is about HER, not about a letter.

going to spend every one of them together and not be seen, and on
Friday they are going to go back to a sheet at five fifteen and

lives and he wants her to have been in it; both want Friday not to
come and say so.*
