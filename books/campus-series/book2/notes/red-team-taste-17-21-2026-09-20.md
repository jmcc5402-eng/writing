## WHAT THE SHEET DOES NOT SAY

1. **Everyone in town talks like the leads.** Sonny, Odell, Peanut,
   Verna: one voice, short and wise, at parts volume. No entry asks
   whether a stranger could tell who is speaking with the names
   covered.
2. **Nobody is ever wrong.** In twenty-four days the only mistake
   anyone makes is half a question (ch20:383). The sheet forgives
   imperfect lines, not imperfect people.
3. **Every question ends in a period.** ch17:413, 434; ch19:319, 327;
   ch21:114. On audio nobody sounds like they want the answer.

## THE ONE CHAPTER

**Ch 18.** The pillow talk (l.378–404) is the book's most intimate
disclosure, hers, told from a barber's chair with a stranger's hand
on his head, and she is not in the scene. It must be its own scene in
room nine, in the dark, with a prompt on the page for why she says
it; Odell keeps the clippers and the count of eleven days, and loses
the bay rum and the crappie. PROPOSED to the author.
