**Score: SAID 0 · MISSED 5 · NEW 3.** The proxy's eight comments were
three feeling/conflict and five nuts-and-bolts, and every one of the
five was fixed before the author read — so the author's read had
none of those to make, which is the half that worked. The half that
did not: the proxy passed POINT on a page the author found toothless,
and it never put a number on the romance or the heat, so it could
not disagree with the panel's 6.

**What changed the same day (author-proxy 1.1.0; L062–L064):** romance
and heat rated on the author's anchors; the stakes-blatant line;
