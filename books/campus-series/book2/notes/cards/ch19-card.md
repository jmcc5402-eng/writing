# Chapter 19 — The Portal

Aisha, Monday the fourth of January. One drafter.

**The plot.**

The portal opened Saturday and the town is home. By noon fifty-six,
the shoulder she ran in chapter 2, is in it. She has his complete
record in an envelope with his name on it before he asks, and she
wishes him well. Boyd walks her rooms at the annex uninvited, with
his consultant's clipboard, and admires her packing. On his way to
the door he passes one remark: her name came up for a directorship
out west. He passes names along. He'd hate to lose her.

**Aisha.**

She is the one person in the building glad to see a kid leave with
everything he is owed, against her own history of never being kept.
She does not answer Boyd. She lets Dan carry.

**Dan.**

Seen from her side. He walks in the warm-end door in daylight with
flat boxes from Facilities in his arms, on her side of the building,
where anyone can see him. He did not plan it. Boyd prices it on his
way out without a word.

**The romance.**

Packing as courtship. The week is over and this is what they have
now: him carrying, her taping, the terms holding, nothing said that
the building could hear. The reader has just had the week, so every
box is the week not being said.

**The town, Boyd, the clock.**

The county learns a roster can dissolve in two weeks. Peanut gets
the line: January is when the money says out loud what it whispered
all fall. Ends flat with menace: Boyd's exit, his coat unstirred.

**Your calls.**

1. The trainer's name goes on fifty-six's tape-times page as the one who took every number, or the file stays hers alone.
2. Boyd's remark: she thinks about it once at the end, or it is left where he dropped it.
3. Peanut's line at the Checkerboard in the morning, or at the Mule's rail that night.
