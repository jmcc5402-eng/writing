# Chapter 19 — The Portal

Aisha, Monday the fourth of January. One drafter.

**The plot.**

The portal opened Saturday and the town is home. By noon fifty-six,
the shoulder she ran in chapter 2, is at her door asking for his
records, because he is in it. She has his file in an envelope with
his name on it before he finishes asking, and wishes him well. Boyd walks her rooms at the annex uninvited, his consultant a
step behind with the survey clipboard, and admires the way her
office is boxing. The tables and the tank are still working. On his way to
the door he passes one remark: her name came up for a directorship
out west. He passes names along. He'd hate to lose her.

**Aisha.**

She is the one person in the building glad to see a kid leave with
everything he is owed, against her own history of never being kept.
She does not answer Boyd. She lets Dan carry.

**Dan.**

Seen from her side. He walks in the warm-end door in daylight with flat
boxes in his arms, on her side of the building, where anyone can
see him. Facilities never sent any; these are the Fieldhouse's. He did not plan it. Boyd prices it on his
way out without a word.

**The romance.**

Packing as courtship. The week is over and this is what they have
now: him carrying, her taping, the terms holding, nothing said that
the building could hear. Every box is the week not being said.

**The town, Boyd, the clock.**

Peanut gets
the line: January is when the money says out loud what it whispered
all fall. Ends flat with menace: Boyd's exit across the lot, and nothing
about him moves.

**Your calls.**

1. The trainer's name goes on fifty-six's file for the numbers he took between hers, so the next school knows who to call, or the file stays hers alone.
2. Boyd's remark: she thinks about it once at the end, or it is left where he dropped it.
3. Peanut's line at the Mule's rail that night, with her there alone for once, or Peanut's line reaches her secondhand and she stays home.
