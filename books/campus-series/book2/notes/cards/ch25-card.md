# Chapter 25 — Boxes

Aisha, Tuesday the twenty-sixth of January. Three blind drafters.

**Targets.**

Romance 6 · Heat 3 · Aisha 3 · Dan 1 · Wound 3 · Fun 1 · Town 3 · Menace 1 · Ends down · Talk quiet · Words 3400 · Pays Aisha

**The plot.**

Day six of the review's fortnight. The offer comes in writing. A
directorship out west, a department with her name over it, a
building nobody is going to box. Answer by Saturday the sixth of
February. Boyd put her name in that pipe so she would go. She packs her office in
an afternoon because she never unpacked a life here. The whole six
months fits back in the car it came in. She carries Verna's twelve
towels down to the washers herself, folded in thirds, the count
square. Mt. Nebo's February program hands her the county's other
memory of the annex. Back in nine she rereads the
Cordelia letter for the first time since the wall. Then the town
starts doing small things nobody asked for. Coffee on the house. The
coach's slice at her elbow, unasked. Verna not charging February.
She chooses the offer anyway.
She folds the staff coat flat on the top box like a flag.

**Aisha.**

Turned. Every account settled before she goes, which is the wound.
Verna's arrangement honored to the letter, which is the kindness. She
leaves because nobody asked her to stay.

**Dan.**

Not in her chapter. The slice at her elbow is the town's. The phone is
in her hand twice and she does not call.

**The romance.**

Apart, and she is the one leaving. The one sentence he did not say at
the window is the one she packs around.

**The town, the clock.**

Eight days to the findings and signing day. Eleven to the answer. The
town's counter-current starts here and pays at 28. Verna's anchor:
boxes pack fast when nobody's asked you to stay.
