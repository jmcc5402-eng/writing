# Chapter 22 — The Whip Count

Dan, Thursday the fourteenth of January. One drafter.

**Targets.**

Romance 5 · Heat 2 · Aisha 1 · Dan 2 · Wound 1 · Fun 1 · Town 3 · Menace 2 · Ends flat · Talk normal · Words 3200 · Pays Dan

**The plot.**

The storm's second day. The hill is still glass, her car is still
under ice at the annex. The portal window shuts tonight. On the board the letter's
follow-up finishes: the copies to the trustees, a folder of receipts,
an email going Friday, all of it polite. The letter itself has sat on
the athletic director's desk since December. Coach's Table goes on the
air from Delmar's porch anyway. A caller
asks for clarity. Dan gives it unasked: the program has the best in
the league and follows her protocol. He says everything but her name,
and the county hears both. Off the air he walks to the northeast corner
where Denny Gault stands, asks after Trey by name, and says nothing
about any letter. Tick tells him what signing day is. That night, for the
first time in ten years, he does not open the board.

**Dan.**

Defends her on the air as process and stops one word short. Stands
in Denny's corner where nobody has stood with him since December. Loses the
board's next sentence and does not read it.

**Aisha.**

Seen once, at dusk, crossing the square as he parks at its corner.
One look. Neither lifts a hand. The county sees a woman walking.

**The romance.**

Apart, and it costs. He says why once: the terms, the post this
morning, three weeks less a day.

**The town, the clock.**

The copies go Friday. Signing day is the third of February. The
extension sits on the same agenda. Boyd is on the porch with a plate,
smiling, saying one thing that is not a threat. Ends flat, with dread, at a
counter with people in the room.

**Your calls.**

1. Boyd is on the porch with a plate and one line off the air, or he is not there and the county only feels him.
2. Her one look is across the square as he parks at its corner, or she is at the quiet end of the rail and gone before the bulb lights.
3. The last section is the Checkerboard counter with the cook and Sonny, or Delmar's porch emptying with Tick.
