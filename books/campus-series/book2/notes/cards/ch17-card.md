# Where we are — Chapter 17 of 30 (week 6 of 11)

Aisha, December 20 to 26. One drafter.

## The plot

The town emptied the morning after the game. Christmas is loud for
the married and long for everybody else, and Aisha stayed with the
clinic's line forwarded to her cell. The Gaults drive to Birmingham
to prove her wrong, and she sends that office her whole chart the
same afternoon, unasked. On the last night of the week the motel is
empty and the two of them have a whole night for the first time.
The second heat scene: it runs up to the first piece of clothing on
the floor, slowly, and cuts there.

## Aisha

She gets the thing she stopped expecting, in the dark, on terms, and
the firewall holds without either of them touching it. Early in the
week, before any of that, she gives thirty-four her stool at the
Checkerboard till January, breakfasts paid, and nobody thanks her.

## Dan

Seen from her side. "Review" is the county's word for his contract
now. He walks down the hill to a lit room because she left it lit,
knowing that if anyone sees, it is the same story as ten years ago.

## The romance

The terms are five days old. The signal has no poles, so it is her
light this time. The night gets the room it needs: the walk to each
other, the looks, the hands, the pauses, the things two serious
people say wrong. Nothing about the call. Ends up.

## Boyd, the town, the clock

Boyd's crews chalk the Millrow curbs in the empty town. Cal and
Marisol at the Checkerboard counter, warm. Verna sees the light and
says nothing, ever. Six weeks to the first Wednesday in February.
