# Chapter 23 — The Album

Aisha, Monday the eighteenth of January. Three blind drafters.

**Targets.**

Romance 6 · Heat 3 · Aisha 2 · Dan 1 · Wound 3 · Fun 0 · Town 3 · Menace 3 · Ends down · Talk normal · Words 3400 · Pays Aisha

**The plot.**

The thaw. The roads are open. Somebody
went through four hundred photos in the parents' board's playoff album
to find one. Her laugh, his lean over the sign, the flash off the ice
chest, the timestamp: eleven in the morning on the nineteenth of
December, the day of the hold. It goes up in the gossip room first,
without names, the way that room talks. By noon a parent posts it
plainly on the parents' board under her own name with the accusation
in one sentence. The room piles on for form and screenshots it into
every side chat in the county. Marisol has to rule on the one post she
cannot delete. Aisha reads it once at six with her boots on and goes
to work. The reader knows who has been reading the archive since
January. Nobody on the page does.

**Aisha.**

Every call she made now reads bought. She sends Marisol one text and
does not spend her friend's son on herself. She does the day's work
with the accusation standing under her name. She does not read it
twice.

**Dan.**

Seen once, or not at all. The same picture is on his phone in his
building, and she knows it.

**The romance.**

Apart, and it costs. The county has handed her the one story better
than the truth, and the picture is true. She looks at her own laugh
and wants what it says. Ends down, on their image side by side on two
phones in two buildings.

**The town, the clock.**

Sixteen days to signing day. The trustees sit in February, with her
job on the agenda under a politer name. A woman who counts boxes
with a pen says a photograph doesn't lie, it repeats whatever it's
told.

3. The anchor line is Delores at the annex with the survey crew, or Verna at her storm door.
