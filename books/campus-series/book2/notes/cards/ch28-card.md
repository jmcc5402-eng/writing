# Chapter 28 — Signing Day

Dan, Wednesday the third of February. Three blind drafters. The false high, then the drop.

**Targets.**

Romance 7 · Heat 3 · Aisha 2 · Dan 2 · Wound 1 · Fun 3 · Town 3 · Menace 1 · Ends down · Talk normal · Words 3400 · Pays Boyd

**The plot.**

Half the county sits in the high school gym for the radio's signing-day
show, though the class signed in December. Dan is there because the
show expects him. The quarterback he flipped in January signs with the
other school, and the two families due today send nothing. Tick reads it
on the air. That is his cost, in public.

Before the broadcast, in the gym's hallway, Dan tells the athletic
director the pledge went with his testimony, not the office. "You
didn't lose the money. I did." Nobody hears it.

Across town the review sits again. Missy Gault stands up with the
Birmingham report she has carried since December, because of what Dan
said Monday. By noon the finding reaches the gym: the call affirmed,
her record clean. The Liars' Table takes its hats off, one man first,
then all of them. Tick says the only signature that mattered this year
was a doctor's.

Cleared, her offer out west still stands. By afternoon the athletic
director has a call from the western chair: she said yes.

**Dan.**

He paid Monday, and today he watches it land. He wanted her cleared
more than he wanted the class. He has still not asked her to stay.

**Aisha.**

Seen only from the gym, and in the news. Cleared, and still packed.

**The romance.**

Apart all day. The town's joy is about her, and he feels it for her.
Then her yes reaches him second-hand.

**The town, the clock.**

The findings come today. Her answer was due Saturday, and she sent it
early. Boyd's survey crew shows up mid-scene.
