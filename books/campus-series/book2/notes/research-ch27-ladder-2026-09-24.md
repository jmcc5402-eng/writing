## 1. The standard ladder

NCAA Concussion Safety Protocol Checklist (June 2026), built on the
Amsterdam 2022 consensus:

1. Symptom-limited daily activity.
2. Aerobic work: 2a light (about 55% of max heart rate), then 2b
   moderate (about 70%). A "mild" (up to 2 points on 0–10) or "brief"
   (under 1 hour) symptom rise is tolerated.
3. Sport-specific work with no head-impact risk. Step 4 only after
   symptoms resolve, including with exertion.
4. Non-contact practice. 5. Unrestricted practice. 6. Return to sport.

"It is typical for each step to be ≥ 24 hours." The team physician
gives final clearance. The checklist says it is "not ... a clinical
practice guideline or legal standard of care." So 24 hours is a typical
minimum, not a speed limit.

## 3. The rule

**Independent Medical Care legislation.** Schools must "affirm the
unchallengeable autonomous authority of primary athletics health care
providers (team physicians and athletic trainers) to determine medical
management and return-to-play decisions." No coach may be a provider's
sole supervisor or hold sole hire/fire power. Its stated aim is
decisions free of influence from "a coach or director of athletics."

- Passed by the five autonomy conferences in January 2016 (Proposal
  2015-15), effective Aug 1, 2016, as Division I Constitution 3.2.4.17.
- The other Division I conferences opted in in October 2016; Divisions
  II and III followed in August 2017.
