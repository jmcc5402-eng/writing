The rebuild worked where it was aimed. Both SAIDs are the two big
questions the author asked the proxy to lead with (conflict, and what
a stake costs), and the proxy's applied comments took the stakes-
blatant and Boyd-named complaints off the author's list entirely.

The three MISSES share a cause. The proxy reads the chapter as a
reader who knows the book's rules. The author reads it as a stranger:
he did not know what "Film's at four" means to a coach, and he did not
know the town room has its own manners. And it has no way to ask
whether a big moment from an earlier chapter is carried here.

The three NEWs are one new want, said three ways: **the argument must
be fair on both sides.** Her call should be arguable, the letter's
signers honest, and some of the town on her side.

**What changes (author-proxy 1.1.1; L091–L095):**
