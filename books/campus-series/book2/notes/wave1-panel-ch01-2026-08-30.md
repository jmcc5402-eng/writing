**CANDIDATE X**

**CANDIDATE Y**

But she stumbles at 639–640 — *"The one that went toe to toe / with Coach at Delores's supper"* — wait, they've already fought? At a supper I didn't see? No DNF point in any candidate — all three end chapter 1 with the reader intact; the closest call is Y at line 640, named above.
