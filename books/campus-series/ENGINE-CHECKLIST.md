# Campus Series — Ingredient Checklist (draft)

**Status: PROPOSED (author, drafting begun 2026-08-08).** The
"beloved aspects" every book delivers. Seeds `02-story-engine.md`
when the series scaffolds from the kit at S1. Adapted from the
city-quartets 17-item draft (`studio/ideas/romance-city-quartets.md`)
plus this series' own machinery.

**Rules of the table (house discipline):**
- **Ingredients are lenses, not scenes.** No item may demand its own
  scene; it colors scenes the plot already needs. If an outline needs
  a new scene to check a box, the box loses.
- Every outline is audited against this table; ABSENT items carry a
  `WAIVED:` proposal or a fix, never a silent drop.
- Numeric/cadence rules here are warnings (waivable per book). Walls
  live in STANDARDS.md (HEA, consent, R1–R2).

| # | Ingredient | The rule of it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet-collision in a campus-town space | bike path, tailgate, comment thread — the town supplies it |
| 2 | One proximity device | logged in the ledger; no back-to-back repeats |
| 3 | One primary trope | rotation-tracked; never repeats as lead within the quartet |
| 4 | A hard institutional clock | on the page EARLY — season, hearing, lease, visa, sunset date |
| 5 | The season's sensory signature | each book owns its quarter's smell/sound/light register |
| 6 | One parent-board beat | invented platform; budgeted; recognition comedy, never contempt |
| 7 | One offstage-kid artifact | a text, voicemail, or empty chair that lands emotionally (R5) |
| 8 | One town-institution beat | neither campus nor chat room — diner, inn, pub, market (standard 21) |
| 9 | A communal set piece | tailgate, festival, quiz night, farmers market |
| 10 | Competence porn | each lead visibly excellent at their real job |
| 11 | Weather forces intimacy or truth | plot, not decor |
| 12 | A food scene | doing emotional work |
| 13 | The lie with a felt cost | planted fair; survives the five-minute honest talk |
| 14 | The grand gesture | spends armor, not money |
| 15 | Non-romantic B-plot won as a team | the set-antagonist arc; earns its pages |
| 16 | The rootable next lead | visible wound; seeded before their book (fractal bench) |
| 17 | Past-couples cameo | the HEA visibly holding |
| 18 | One elder beat | an older love story touched (mega-theme 4) |
| 19 | One matchmaker touch | exactly one, deniable, never load-bearing [TK: pending D01] |
| 20 | Epilogue hand-forward | to the next book; final chapter |
| 21 | One or two side hookups (author, 2026-08-08) | adult townsfolk, low plot weight, reasonable steam — the ensemble IS the point of a college town; all within the locked heat band; R1 applies (adults only) |
| 22 | A dog (author, 2026-08-08) | somewhere in every book, not always the leads' — the diner's, the inn's, a tailgate regular's; rotation rules per book bible; candidate series thread (recurring dogs readers track) |

Heat placement per the locked ladder ([TK: steam definition]) rides
on the outline spine, not this table.
