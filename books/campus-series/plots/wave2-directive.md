## Register rulings (author)

- **10% corny is the point.** This is a warm, openly sentimental
  book. The interruption gag, the town chorus, the pie logic — lean
  in. Corn is budgeted, not banned; wink at the reader, never smirk.
- **Register sample = accepted ch01** (the ruled pilot). Blind rule
  stands: no drafter sees chapters 2–8 or any sibling candidate.
- Old-school country vibe WITH a modern twist — see texture banks.

## Standing gates (wave-1 rejections, now pre-checks)

1. **Dialogue floor:** ≥15% quoted words per chapter (target ~25%);
   linter reports the % with every candidate. **Named-speaker
   share ≥80%** of dialogue turns (no anonymous-chorus blocks
   buying the number).
2. **Caused beats:** the POV lead must CAUSE ≥3 beats per chapter —
   things happen because they act, not around them.
3. **Chorus on page:** named sub-characters participate in every
   chapter; the town is never scenery.
4. **Warmth/rootability + Cal appeal** (the wave-1 pass directives,
   now standing): both leads visibly likable, competent, and
   attractive on the page — looks, strength, humor in frame.
5. **Fun floor:** every chapter earns one laugh and one ache.

- **Cal — deferred maintenance, self-applied.** The widower who

- Their medicine is mutual: he maintains what she never had
  maintained; she witnesses what he never had witnessed.

## The sexy layer (closed door, standard 9 — first-garment line)

## The wine-night calling card (author-stated series device)

Debut: ch7's porch wine night. Once per book, never twice.
- One of the ladies relays a STEAMY secondhand story — "my cousin,"
  "a friend down in Birmingham" — sensory, earthy, funny ("twenty
  minutes on her back, every electrode in her body").
- **The story is ALWAYS interrupted at the crucial syllable** — a
  knock, a cork, a phone lighting up MOM CALLING. The interruption
  is the hook; the room groans; different interruption every book.
- "Tell it proper" is the in-world rule (sensory specifics
  demanded); deniability is the game (everyone pretends to believe
  the cousin exists).
- The told story obeys the same first-garment line as lived scenes.
  Subjects always adults (R1 applies inside stories), never
  identifiable real people, never affairs (standard 12).
