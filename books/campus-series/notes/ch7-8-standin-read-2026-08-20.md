## 2. The set-level audit — the complaint is systemic, with a precise shape

**It is not that bodies are absent. The set's body language is a
monoculture of hands and competence.** Ch 5 was drafted under the
hands-and-objects card; ch 8's brief rules *"competence is the heat
source"*; every notice-budget gate was satisfiable with hands. The
system got exactly what it asked for: forearms, thumbs on wood, hands
"quick, certain, ringless."

What no chapter since ch 1 has: a smile between the leads, an eye
described, a mouth, hair doing anything, either lead's face reacting
to the other. **Ch 1 spent the whole account** — *"something happened
at the corner of his mouth that a generous witness might have logged
as almost a smile"*; the door-opening smile; *"It was a good mouth.
She had time to notice, and used it"* — **and chapters 5–8 never
reopened it.** A first-timer starting at ch 5 could not tell you one
thing about either lead's face, and can tell you plenty about a wood
plane.

**Root cause is the briefs, not the drafters. The fix belongs in the
gate:** amend the notice-budget from "≥2 physical-notice beats" to
**"≥2, of which at most one may be hands/competence — the other must
be face, mouth, hair, or body-in-clothes."** Cap at 4 per chapter;
past four, the closed-door register starts reading like a different
heat band, and the restraint is the brand.

**The set's existing swoon inventory** (protect while grafting):
ch5:56–59 (*"She gave herself one second to notice and spent
three"*); ch5:180–181 (*"His hands, for once, were doing nothing at
all"* — the best Cal sentence in the set); ch5:224–227 (the shoulders
reorganizing her opinion of Sundays); ch6's dress with opinions and
its closing statement; **ch6:290–291 — the ONLY face-level compliment
in four chapters, and SHE pays it** (*"the gray at your temples backs
up every number you say"*) — **Cal owes one back and the book hasn't
paid it**; ch7's watched laugh and the hands recalled down to the curl
of shaving; ch8's screw fix, glance, handshake.

## 4. The ration

- **Signature tells:** max one firing per tell per chapter, 5–6 per
  book, each at a turn. Never as filler; never two tells in one beat.
- **Chorus naming a tell:** once per book per tell. Twice is a bit.
- **Full smiles between the leads:** Cal's door-open smile, 3 per book
  (ch 1 spent one). **Marisol's direct-at-him smile: withheld entirely
  until the T06 payoff** — the withholding the whole quirk economy is
  saving up for.
- **Per-scene attraction notices:** floor 2 per chapter (composition
  amended per §2), cap 4.
- If the author wants a lighter touch, the third candidate on each
  slate is the cuttable one. **Load-bearing: T06's payoff structure
  and the corner-of-mouth.** The rest is charm.
