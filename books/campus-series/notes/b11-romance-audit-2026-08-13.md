## Findings

F1 [HIGH] — The ch 11–15 dead zone. Five consecutive chapters
with the couple apart, directly after the ch 10 vulnerability
high. Absence powers the ch 16 reunion, but five is past the
three-chapter tolerance; a romance reader stalls at ch 13.
Fix (cheapest): put Marisol at the ch 12 fieldwork day — she is
Friends chair with walkthrough access (established ch 9), so her
presence at the east-wall photography is procedurally earned. One
charged, mostly wordless beat; keep Walt's tell-tale check alone
afterward so the ch 16 reveal is preserved. Alt: Walt and crew
deliver folding tables at ch 13's Fifth Sunday (crew presence
already in-scene via Deke) — costs a little of the circuit's
Marisol-only texture.

F2 [HIGH] — No dark moment on the relationship. The outline's
proud line "the conflict survives the honesty" is SC4 gold, but
after ch 16 the bond is never tested from inside; ch 22–23's
all-is-lost is entirely civic. The reader is never afraid of
losing the couple.
Fix (cheapest): reprice the ch 24 call and the ch 25 consent.
Ch 24 currently lands triumphant; make it costly — going public
confirms "coordination," her correction outs his grief and their
kiss, his clearing deflates her surge. One on-page beat (ch 24
call or a short ch 25 consent scene) where each offers to let the
other go to spare them the cost — and both refuse. Dark moment on
the bond, zero misunderstanding, SC4 intact, no new chapter.

F3 [MEDIUM] — The ch 2–5 opening run. Four plot chapters after a
single hostile meeting; second contact waits until ch 6.
Fix: a thirty-second micro-encounter inside ch 5 — Walt at the
Curb Market (a crew parts/flower run is already in-scene via
Deke), reading the clipboard, not signing, her watching. It also
attaches a face to her ch 5 realization that the report, not
opinion, is the fight.

F4 [MEDIUM] — Pre-midpoint heat is all subtext. Grump/sunshine at
mid steam needs on-page wanting before the kiss. Fix: one line of
attraction interiority per POV in Q1–Q2 — Marisol has hers (ch 7,
"watched the person laugh"); give Walt an equivalent, cheapest in
ch 14, where "tells no one — not Marisol, not the crew" should
put her name first and make the not-telling ache.

F5 [LOW] — Together-count 13/30 is a floor, not a failure; F1 and
F3 raise it to 15/30 and, more importantly, fix distribution. No

## VERDICT

This is a romance, not a zoning drama with kissing — the proof is
that every structural pillar (meet, spark, midpoint, PONR, dark
night, climax, HEA) is a couple scene, and the civic machinery is
honestly load-bearing per SC4: the pen IS the man. But the
middle-out build shows its seams exactly where the brief feared:
16 of 30 chapters move machinery while the relationship stands
still, with a four-chapter and a five-chapter run where the leads
never touch the same page, and an all-is-lost that never once
threatens the couple. The minimum repair is four beats inside
existing scenes — Marisol at the ch 12 fieldwork, a market
micro-encounter in ch 5, a costly ch 24/25 consent beat that puts
the bond itself on the table, one line of Walt's wanting in ch 14
— which converts the two dead zones and supplies the missing
relationship dark moment without adding a chapter or disturbing
the fair-play table.
