## 7. THE PROCESS FINDING

Five blocking errors in ~86 sentences is roughly one per seventeen. All
five are **seam** errors — a line asserting something true of the book
but false of the page it was dropped onto — which is precisely the
class the missing audit addendum exists to catch, and precisely what
card E4 was pointed at.

**BACKLOG item:** the studio has no brief template for **retrofit runs
on accepted prose**. A drafter entering a folded chapter needs the
chapter's own facts handed to it — the clock, the blocking, the
established habits, the graded codes (Ratchet's tail) — because
whole-book knowledge is exactly what makes it write a line the page
cannot support.
