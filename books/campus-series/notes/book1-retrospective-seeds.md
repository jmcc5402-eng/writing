# Book 1.1 — Retrospective seeds (for the Book 2 planning retro)

Questions deliberately PARKED during Book 1 production, per the
author (2026-08-21, in chat): "Whether it's a bug or Gilmore girls,
we should proceed. The beauty of having a whole series is that we
can do retrospective on book one for book 2… It very well could be
that slow churn leads is something people love."

Rule: items here are NOT fixed in Book 1 and NOT re-argued
mid-production. They get answered once, with Book 1's full text and
(if available) real reader reaction in hand, and the answers feed
Book 1.2's premise and briefs.

1. **Chorus sparkle vs lead sparkle.** Panel MVP is Earlene; the
   rooms readers most want back are chorus rooms (Delmar's, wine
   night). Bug, or the Gilmore engine working? Wave-3+ briefs hold
   the line ("leads get the best line in shared scenes"); the retro
   decides the series-level dial.
2. **The slow-burn dial.** Quarter pole = two handshakes and a
   pocketed place card. The four-star reviewer "docks one star as a
   hostage negotiation tactic"; the author's hypothesis: slow churn
   may be exactly what this readership loves. Judge with the whole
   book — the burn's back half (ch 16 first HEAT, ch 30) exists by
   then.
3. **The two-star reader.** "This is a book about a TOWN and a
   HOUSE" — she's the cost of the promise the package makes.
   Retro question: is the blurb/packaging honest enough that she
   self-selects out BEFORE buying?
4. **Ch 2's first third** (the work-order stretch) — the one coast
   in eight chapters, home of the hypothetical DNF. Worth a trim in
   the pre-publication polish batch, or part of the town-first
   promise?

Add seeds here as they surface; nothing in this file is a Book 1
work item.
