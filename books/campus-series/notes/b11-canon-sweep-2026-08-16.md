# PART 2 — THE OTHER BLOCKERS

## B6 — Jurisdiction: the House's fate is decided by the wrong body

`town-ashford.md` §5 is explicit: Hartwell House is **trust-owned, on
Millrow, off-campus**; the university is immune from town zoning *on
its own campus*, and Boyd's footprint "spills across Millrow into
trust-owned and private town land," requiring street vacation,
rezoning, a development agreement and a redevelopment resolution —
each a **Board of Aldermen** vote. The census says that is the vote
that beats Boyd, **and it is Book 1.4's plot.**

The frozen outline stakes everything on a **university facilities
director's signature** condemning a building the university does not
own, resolved at a **university trustees' session**. Under the ratified
census the university can neither condemn the trust's house nor grant
it a stay.

**This is the premise sentence.** Three ways out, all needing a
ruling: (a) recast the finding as a *university condition survey*
whose real power is over Boyd's gift and the crew's winter, and
downgrade the "stay" to what trustees can actually deliver; (b) put
the House on university-leased land (amends the census); (c) amend the
census's jurisdiction section.
