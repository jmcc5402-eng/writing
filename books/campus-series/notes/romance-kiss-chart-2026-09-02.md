## 4. WHAT THE CHART SHOWS

1. **The book opens on somebody else's kiss.** Chapter one, page one, an
   anonymous couple on a porch, in front of whistling neighbors — fifteen
   chapters before the leads touch. It sets the contract and buys the long dry run.
2. **The leads kiss five times in thirty chapters**, and each one is a different
   kind: private (16), reckoned-with (23), public-on-purpose (29), and vowed (30).
   No kiss repeats another kiss's job.
3. **The invented kiss is the load-bearing one.** ch 25 → ch 29 is the book's
   cleverest romance move: the town's lie becomes the leads' choice.
4. **Two live secondary romances end the book unfinished** — Eli/Birdie and
   Verna's suitor. Both are deliberate series fuel. Aisha's is the third, and
   Book 1.2 is spending it.
5. **Both leads carry a closed marriage in.** Hers ended by divorce, his by death.
   Neither is ever staged as competition with the other.
