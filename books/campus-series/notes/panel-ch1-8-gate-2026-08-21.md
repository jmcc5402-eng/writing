## 3. THE WANTING CURVE — hungrier at 8 than at 4? Yes, unambiguously.

**First want:** ch 1, "It was a good mouth. She had time to notice, and used it." **Wants it most:** ch 8's held look — "it ran one beat past the place where either of them could have called it an accident" — followed by the handshake: "He let go on time, and it cost him something."

**Escalation, not plateau** — but note the shape: on-page couple contact happens in ch 1, 5, 6, 8 only. Ch 2–4 is a three-chapter contact gap that the wrench tether ("a debt like that kept a man findable," ch 3) just barely keeps warm. From ch 5 on, every meeting escalates in publicness and charge, and the book's two withholding engines compound: the unreturned wrench and the never-given direct smile.

**Denied on purpose vs. by accident:** the withheld smile is denial on purpose and it is working. The wrench is currently read as on-purpose too — but the wave-3 directive itself flags (flag 1) that "I'll bring you the right one" has **no payoff slot in the frozen outline through ch 12**. A spoken promise the reader has held since ch 1, carried un-acknowledged past the midpoint, tips from tease into accident. Reporting, not prescribing: the outline-rung decision the directive asks for is the difference.

**The quirk layer: character, not decoration — but only as of ch 8.** Honest finding: through ch 1, 5, and 7, Marisol's past-the-ear smile read to three of four panelists as a repeated tic; only the review-writer clocked it as deliberate. Then ch 8 detonates it: "Every smile Cal had ever gotten off that woman had gone on past his ear, and he had not counted that a loss until right now." That single line converts the entire run retroactively into the book's best want-engine — the reader now consciously craves the straight-on smile, which per the directive stays withheld. That is a preorder mechanism. Cal's corner-of-mouth (ch 6 "went somewhere without clearing it with him first"; ch 8 "went somewhere it hadn't been invited, and the porch saw it go") reads as character throughout — control cracking in front of witnesses, rationed and therefore valuable. The panel notes the ration is now spent per the directive; the reader's appetite for the next crack is part of the pull.
