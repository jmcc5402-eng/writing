# Scene-break instrument — Book 1.1, all 30 chapters (2026-09-03)

**Scope note first, because it changes what this report is.** The premise ("zero scene-break markers — no `***`, no `---` inside a chapter") does not match the files at `/home/user/writing/books/campus-series/manuscript/`. Twenty-two of the thirty chapters already carry intra-chapter markers: 43 `---` lines and 10 `***` lines, 53 in all. The example given (ch24, shop to parts house) already has a `---` at line 148. So either the orchestrator's view is stale or the markers were dismissed because `---` is not the house form (`studio/STYLE.md` specifies `***`). I have therefore done two things in one table: audited every existing marker against the time/place/POV test, and proposed the breaks that are missing. Each row says which it is. Five chapters (04, 08, 16, 20, 28) are single continuous scenes and warrant nothing.

On transitions (card E4): the book enters almost every scene on a clock — "By nine," "at a quarter past ten," "The carillon struck noon," "Thursday night" — and exits on a settled object (a mug, a wrench on a rag, a dog on her stencil). That convention is what makes most of the calls below easy: where a clock-stamp opens a paragraph and the location has changed, the break is HIGH. The hard calls are all the same species — a clock-stamp advances an hour *inside* one location, and an existing marker sits there.

Convention: **After line** is the last prose line of the scene; the marker (where present) sits two lines below it, and the next scene's first line is two lines below that. Quotes are ≤60 characters and may be the tail of a longer line.
