## 1. The carried-over collisions

### Stadium capacity — the prose is compliant; the outline is not

`:269` commits to *"ninety thousand people breathing in unison,"*
which descends correctly from the outline (ninety thousand twice) and
the brief. **The collision is outline-internal and unsurvivable:** ch
26 says *"seventy thousand seats of nobody."* A 70,000-seat stadium
cannot hold 90,000 people.

The census/energy-bank 100,000 is a **town** figure, not a seat
figure, so it doesn't compete — 90,000 in the bowl plus residents plus
the RV city lands near it comfortably.

**Cost of changing it: three edits today** (`ch01.md:269`,
`b11-outline-rev2.md:87` and `:209`). That is the cheapest this
decision will ever be. It gets expensive at three thresholds: ch 26's

chapter title and the closing line. **Recommend keeping ninety and
amending ch 26.** Ninety supports the third-largest-city claim;
seventy does not.
