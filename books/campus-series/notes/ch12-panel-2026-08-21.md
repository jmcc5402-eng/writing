## 1. RANKING

**WINNER: Candidate A** (`/home/user/writing/books/campus-series/drafts/2026-08-21/ch12-candidate-A.md`)

A wins three of the four VISION criteria and loses the first by only a nose. The slides scene — criterion 2, the chapter's named emotional peak — is not close. "He stood in front of that wall a long time. Longer than checking takes." through "the sound came down Millrow the same as it came every day, and let him off" is the ache the directive ordered: grief measured, not information delivered. The ink "gone tea-colored up near the crown molding and darker toward the floor, laid down season by season in the years she kept the watch" makes nineteen years a *physical gradient on a wall* — the 10 p.m. reader put the book down on her chest here, and that's the good kind of putting down.
