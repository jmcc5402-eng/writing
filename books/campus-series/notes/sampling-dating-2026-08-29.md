## THE SEAM REPORT

Dating was not near-impossible. It was **moderately easy**, and that is the finding: this book has a detectable rhythm gradient a sharp reader will feel around the two-thirds mark even if they never name it.

**What changes, and in which direction:**

1. **Sentence economy tightens monotonically.** Early waves (D02, D08, D10) run long, loose, cumulative sentences with comic personification and zero fragments. Late waves (D14, D15, D16) run short declarative volleys, tolerate fragments, strand single sentences for effect, and end scenes on withheld beats. D09 and D05 sit visibly mid-transition. **Verdict: DESIGN.** This is the classic shape of a book compressing as stakes rise, and it tracks the emotional arc too cleanly to be accidental. If the drafters didn't plan it, they got lucky in the same direction eight times.

2. **Rhetorical patterning (anaphora, parallel verdicts, echo-closers) intensifies late.** D01 and D14 are near-oratory. **Verdict: mostly DESIGN, with a DRIFT warning** — by the final third, nearly every paragraph closes on a turned phrase ("and he noticed that he noticed," "her half of the nothing," "gave the shop dog her due before...," "the record didn't need him to"). The device is present in all three thirds — which is what keeps the seams from gaping — but its *density* climbs until the late book risks sounding like it's always concluding. That escalation reads like rotating drafters each imitating the house closer and adding ten percent. Readers will feel late chapters as "quotable but airless."

3. **Interiority migrates from comic-observational to moral-elegiac.** Early: she declines to itemize the looking. Late: "Nobody in the county had laid a hand on the truth of her." **Verdict: DESIGN** — appropriate deepening — but the early register's playfulness disappears almost entirely after the October beat. A seamless book would let one late paragraph grin. None here does; D13 comes closest and it's warmth, not wit.

4. **The one genuinely seamless element:** trade-mechanical simile discipline ("valve feathered," "generators clearing their throats," "voted with glassware") is constant across all sixteen. Whoever enforced that diction rule did the real voice-unification work. Keep that rule; it's the book's fingerprint and it doesn't drift.

**Highest-leverage fix for the top objection (the compression gradient reading as a seam):** don't flatten the gradient — it's earning its keep — but *seed the late rhythm early*. Two or three stranded short sentences and one fragment in the first third (and, symmetrically, one long ambling cumulative sentence in the final third) converts a detectable step-change into a designed crescendo. The cheapest single edit: give the opening-third chapters one D16-style withheld beat apiece, so silence is established as this narrator's move before the book needs it to carry the climax.

**Verdict: CLOSE.** The drift runs in one coherent direction and mostly reads as intention; a trade reviewer would call the late book "increasingly assured" rather than "inconsistent." But the closer-density pileup in the final third and the total extinction of the early comic register are drafter-rotation artifacts a line pass could erase in a week. Fix those two and dating this book from prose alone should drop from moderately easy to genuinely hard — which is the standard.
