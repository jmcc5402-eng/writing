### The structural reason

**`books/campus-series/THREADS.md` does not exist.** The workspace
`CLAUDE.md` requires one per book — *"a per-chapter ledger of what
each chapter introduces, carries, pays off, and hands forward…
`OWED:` marks a plant awaiting its payoff"* — and specifies that
*"drafting agents get the relevant index entries in their brief."*

**Nine drafters wrote chapters 2–4 with no ledger of what chapter one
owed.** Cal's spoken wrench promise, the clock tower, the board's
voice and Marisol's tell would all have been `OWED:` rows. **This is
the highest-leverage missing must-have in the run.**
