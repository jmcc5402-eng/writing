# MYBYB — Reboot Concept (North Star)

> This is the current source of truth for the reboot. The original 2014–2018 manuscript
> (`manuscript/MYBYB-master.md`) is now **raw ore** — we mine it for ideas, not voice.

## Logline

A gift-book-sized, gleefully funny survival guide for your first decade in the workforce — built on one liberating secret: **the bar is on the floor, and beating your beatable boss is going to be kind of fun.**

## The thesis (and the reframe that powers it)

**Old (2014):** "Here's how to cope with / manage your incompetent boss." — reactive, political, a survivor's mindset.

**Reboot:** "Your boss is probably faking it. That's not oppression — it's an *opportunity*, and an achievable one. Here's how to aggressively take control of your first decade and have fun doing it."

The reader is not a victim managing upward. They're an operator who just noticed the final boss has a health bar.

## Tone — the single most important thing

**Amused confidence, not anger.** The comedy engine is *status*: the reader looks **down** at a comically beatable obstacle and laughs, never **up** at an oppressor.

- ✅ Deadpan, precise, savage-about-incompetence wit.
- ✅ "Loser boss as an achievable goal to overcome" — beatable video-game boss, not a threat.
- ✅ Optimistic aggression. Gleeful. In on a funny secret.
- ❌ "The world is against me." Grievance. Resentment. Whining. Victimhood.
- ❌ Punching down at people, coworkers, or any group.

**The guardrail (keeps it edgy AND safe AND ageless):** the bite always points at *incompetence and broken systems* — never at people or identities.

## Audience & business model

- **Reader:** ~22–30, first decade in the workforce, corporate/hybrid job, cynical-but-ambitious, phone reader.
- **Shock = acquisition engine.** Edgy title + cover + gag/gift purchase earns the buy.
- **Substance = retention engine.** "Bought it as a joke, it's actually the realest career advice I've gotten" → 5-star reviews + word-of-mouth. This surprise-and-delight is the Amazon flywheel.
- **Gift-ability is the #1 sales vector.** Built to be handed to a new grad / between coworkers. Short, ~$9.99–14.99.

## Format

**Gift book.** Short, punchy, screenshot-able chapters — one bitter title, one sharp idea, one story, one move you can run Monday. ~13–15 chapters. Punches way above its length on substance.

No startup how-to (that's a potential spin-off, not a chapter).

**Modern threads woven throughout:** Slack performance-theater, RTO politics, AI quietly eating your tasks (be the one who *wields* it), why "quiet quitting" is the average person's trap.

## The defining nuance: Gas Pedal vs. Ride the Wave

The book is not "beat your boss." It's "**read the situation, then pick the move.**" This is the frame that makes the whole thing credible instead of one-note:

- **Hit the gas** when your boss is an *anchor* (a faker going nowhere): overcome, route around, outshine. — most of the book.
- **Ride the wave** when your boss is a *boat* (competent, ambitious, climbing): amplify them on purpose, backfill them, get towed up the wake.

Riding the wave is a **cold, confident bet on a winner — not bootlicking** (which is fear aimed at a loser). It always comes with an exit: the moment the wave flattens, you let go of the rope. This nuance also makes the book giftable *upward* — a decent manager can enjoy it without it being purely anti-boss.
