_Blind run, no variance card by design — a card is a lens, and the

## What the blind agent reconstructed correctly

Worth stating first, because it calibrates everything below: from one
file it recovered the book's thesis almost exactly —

> Work is not a meritocracy run by competent people; it is a field of
> unclaimed vacancies left open by a voluntary "standing-out tax" that
> everyone pays out of fear of being visible, and the way to survive
> your first decade is to stop paying that tax and walk into the
> vacancies for free.

— plus the escalation axis (one problem alone → one room live → one
direction upward → one person permanently, with the price rising from
effort to being right to owning the gap to credit), the shape of ch. 4
as a menu chapter, and Part III's register (other people, direction,
whose wave you ride and when to let go of the rope). It also produced
a Part paragraph and four chapter entries that read as jacket copy
rather than shorthand.

**That is the good news and it is substantial:** the expansion carries
its argument. What it does not carry is a single consistent account of
its own price, its own low point, and its own artifacts.

## DRIFT — internal contradictions (fix at rung 3, before anything else)

**D1. The Part cannot decide whether the moves are free.** Three
incompatible pricings coexist:
- one-sentence: "Four moves… **each one free**"
- contrarian comp: "at zero cost, **and the fourth one** costs you
  something real"
- ch. 5 prose: "the honest turn, and it is **the first one in the
  book**: this move is not free" — plus Block 3, which prices all four.

Block 2 compounds it by assigning the free-inversion to ch. 8
("ch. 4's 'one price… zero' inverts in ch. 8's credit-for-control
trade") when ch. 5 already performs that inversion three chapters
early. **A drafter reading top-down will write ch. 5 as free.** This
is the most consequential single fix.

**D2. Two chapters both claim the Part's low point.** Ch. 7's turn is
"the setback the whole Part has been walking toward"; ch. 8's turn "is
the Part's low point on purpose." Block 2 resolves it structurally
(ch. 7 = 75% setback, ch. 8 = 90% climax) — which leaves ch. 8 a
climax described in prose as a low point. Risk: two consecutive
troughs.

**D3. The vacuum callback is double-homed.** Block 6 rations "walk
into the vacuum" to ch. 5, once. Block 8 says T10 owes ch. 7 the
vacuum question at project scale, and ch. 7's prose spends it. Either
"walk into the vacuum" and "the vacuum question" are two separate ch.
1 assets, or the ration is violated. **Needs a LEXICON ruling.**

**D4. "The smallest room to the most dangerous one" contradicts the
venue roster.** The one-sentence progresses by room size; Block 5 runs
thread → meeting room → meeting room → one-on-one, and the one-on-one
is the *smallest* room and comes last. The real axis is exposure and
permanence. Fix: drop the room-size claim from the Part sentence.

**D5. "Each one performed in public" vs. ch. 5's "semi-public."**
Block 3 lists ch. 5 as "You, one problem, semi-public / Who has to
agree: Nobody"; ch. 5's prose says the cost is "work, in public."

**D6. The Olympic Rings are five; the room has eight people.** The
Part paragraph, ch. 6's prose and Block 9 all fix the meeting at eight
("eight people each holding a different ring") against a branded
five-ring asset. Block 9's own one-metaphor rule means a drafter
literalizes this immediately. No guidance anywhere in the file.

**D7. F5 is contradicted by a claim the same file calls
load-bearing.** F5: "No chapter in Part II may require the boss to be
a faker." Block 8 marks "Nobody else in the room knows either" as
resting on "the faker above you, the fake scoreboard" and rules it
"Supported — load-bearing"; ch. 7's central arithmetic builds on "the
person above you is at the job they are worst at." Probable intent: a
*fallible* boss, not a faker. As written, F5 invalidates a claim it
elsewhere approves.

**D8. Are the Monday moves the Cheap Power boxes, or additional?**
The comp section says every chapter "ends with" a Monday move; Block 2
calls "the four Monday-move boxes" the outer finish line (box = move).
But Block 7 places the boxes *between* chapters and mismatched to
their content — **The Agenda Nobody Asked For sits after ch. 5 but is
a ch. 6 move**; The Recap Send sits after ch. 7 but is a ch. 8 move.
So either each chapter has an in-chapter move *plus* a forward-teasing
box (8 artifacts), or the boxes are the moves and ch. 5's move is
about meetings. **The agent's own verdict: this is the single most
consequential ambiguity for a drafter, because it changes the shape of
every chapter ending** — and the one where blindness cost most (a
THREADS read would have settled it).

**D9. Block 4's binary is bent by fiat.** The rule is "each chapter
must reduce to the standing-out tax or it is a rung-3 finding"; ch. 8
"reduces, and then deliberately exceeds" it. There is no "exceeds"
verdict in the test. Probably sanctioned by CONCEPT.md, which the
blind agent could not read — but the test as written does not permit
its own outcome.

**D10. Ch. 5's claim to a unique instrument is false against the
file.** "The only tool in Part II that is a tool rather than a nerve"
— but the Rings are an ordered procedure, BIU is a script with three
sample sentences, and ch. 8 supplies BLUF and CFC. Intended
distinction is probably diagnostic instrument vs. behaviors.

**D11. Locked vs. movable on ch. 8's terms.** Block 6 marks NR
"Locked (reserved)" at ch. 8; OQ 4 proposes moving NR to Part III. A
term cannot be both locked to a home and a live relocation candidate.

**D12 (structural risk, not a contradiction). OQ 7 spends the
ch. 6/ch. 7 anti-blur device.** Block 5 separates the two chapters by
clock position in the same room — ch. 6 at minute thirty-four, ch. 7
at minute two — and states plainly that "without it these two chapters
read as one." OQ 7 then proposes opening ch. 6 with the poker table, a
before-the-meeting movement occupying ch. 7's minute-two slot. The
thinnest structural device in the file, and a proposal in the same
file spends it.

## Ambiguities (rulings, not errors)

- **A1.** Ch. 7's ledger row reads "Who has to agree: someone above
  you," but the chapter's point is that the question is worth asking
  *especially* when there is nothing up there — in which case nobody
  above can agree. Mislabeled row, or "must tolerate"?
- **A2.** Where is the standing-out tax actually *defined*? Block 8
  sources it to ch. 4; Block 6 lists it as an already-debuted
  callback. Matters because Block 4 requires all four chapters to
  reduce to it, and the reduction is only legible on the full
  definition.
- **A3.** Ch. 5's IN-state contains a belief about the *book*
  ("Part II is one purchase repeated at a price of zero") that ch. 5
  itself revokes. Designed residual belief, or a promise ch. 4's
  frozen prose makes and the Part breaks? (Ties to D1.)
- **A4.** The "Nobody's auditing." ration is uncomputable as written —
  n=4 across adopted text "and one flagged double-spend," with no way
  to tell whether the remaining allowance is one or zero, or whether
  the double-spend was already deducted. *(Orchestrator's note: the
  double-spend was repaired the same morning — ch03's box now reads
  "Nobody's checking," and ch04's second use is cut. The phrase now
  has exactly one home, ch04:43. That resolves A4: **zero remaining
  for Part II** unless the author reopens it.)*
- **A5.** Reader tenure is undetermined — the inner journey implies an
  individual contributor waiting to be noticed; ch. 8 assumes an
  annual review and a boss's boss. Year one or year seven changes
  ch. 5's decade-clock line materially, and OQ 8 forbids the
  statistics that would anchor it. Blind guess: years 1–3.
- **A6.** Word-budget feasibility is asserted, not tested. Ch. 5 at
  ~1,200–1,300 words owes the tangible/intangible split *performed
  live* (F1, "the single most important obligation"), the pricing
  turn, the decade clock, a literalized metaphor, three rationed
  callbacks, a handoff, and a Monday move. The file names ch. 8's term
  load as a risk (OQ 4) but not ch. 5's, **which looks like the
  heavier of the two.**
- **A7.** "Finding" names two different objects — the fair-play
  obligations F1–F5, and "a rung-3 finding" meaning a failure of the
  tax-reduction test.
