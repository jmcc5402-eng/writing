4 ten book mini series

2\. more advanced skills and more exotic places

formula for good story:

intro - something interesting, either based on a location, a pet, a character,
1-3 atmospherics, 1-3 characteristics of the location

problem and solution - problem that starts from either: a main character, a
locational character,

Obstacles in the way - 3-4 obstacles that make solving the problem difficult

some type of technical tool ((these should be presents from their parents
conferences.))

Start each section with one thing that leads to the point of the section (and
maybe the first section has a clue for a future section)(this is was jk rowly
does)

3-4 clues that lead to the answer

3-4 risks the twins have to take to overcome the obstacles and solve the problem

A point of no return (often the biggest risk)

A major disaster making it seem like the problem won't be solved

Final risk and resolution

secondary plot (could be anything)

2-3 atmospherics in each story:

\- sickness

\- weather

\- holiday

\- recent event in the area

\- sports event

\- new movie coming out

\- first time for something (either excitement or fear)

\- parents decision

\- new toy or gadget

\- pet event (dog is sick, giving dog a bath, cats are lost)

Types of obstacles:

\- weather or natural event: rain, snow, earthquake (less often), flooding,
   tsunami

\- mistake made by main character - forgot to bring something, got angry and
   someone got mad, broke something by mistake,

\- something goes missing - stolen, lost, left

\- pet or other animal does something - dog eats the paper with the answer, bees
   are flying outside the building,

\- physical block - locked door, fence, moat, large lawn

\- parents decision - can't go out at night, must get to bed earl, need to stay
   home and read,

\- we don't even know who? (who is the bad guy? this might be the whole plot, or
   just part of it)

Random good plot ideas:

\- takes off shoes at beach but is then stuck barefoot.

\- relationship with parents? -

Problems (plots):

\- animals in the zoo are being let and/or stolen

\- teacher supplies are going missing and the kids can't learn (maybe in poor
   country)

\- the local water has gone bad, no one knows why (maybe no bad guy, or natural
   issue, or animals are cuasing)

\- sports mascot gone missing (possibly too cliche)

\- sports team loses their star player (went camping and got lost in woods?)

\- missing person in general (child is lost)

\- Extortion? someone taking money from local business, but no one will be a
   witness (twins meet a kid whose parents are being extorted, kids use spy
   cameras to catch him in the act and stop the extortion)

\- jealosy - one character covets something someone else has (how did the
   wedding cake episode of nancy drew finish? was there a bad guy?);

\- fraud - something gets destroyed and someone else is framed

\- hacking: can be used for many of the above problems, or a stand alone problem

always a secondary plots:

\- dog isn't eating

\- learning to do the laundry

\- having trouble with homework

\- favorite sports team lost
