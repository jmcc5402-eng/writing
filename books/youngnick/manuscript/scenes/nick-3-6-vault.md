**Nick 3.6 Nick checks out the vault**

Once the boat left, Nick rushed over to the vault concealed in the cliff. He
moved away the shrubs and viewed the locked door. How could he open it?

Without any other options available, Nick ventured towards the cabin. He might
get lucky and find a key.

It occurred to Nick that the Grins may not have been alone. He crept around the
back of the cabin and peered through a window. The place seemed deserted. After
observing for a few minutes, Nick entered through a back window.

**((describe cabin))**

Nick quietly searched a number of cabinets and shelves. Nothing. He entered a
room in the rear of the cabin that contained a desk, the same model as the desk
at the mine cabin! Could this one also have a secret compartment? Under the desk
was a latch, after moving it to the side, a drawer opened with a key inside.

Nick dropped the key in his pocket and bolted out the door, returning to the
vault in the cliff. Twisting the key into the rusting lock, Nick winced as the
vault door screeched open. This wasn’t just a closet, but an entire room.
Similar to the hidden compartment in the mine house, but this was much bigger.
The Grins, possibly the Grin’s father, had planned a lot of secrecy into this
mine. ‘What for?’, Nick wondered.

The room was lined with crates. ((Describe the valut))

Nick focused on a crate in the middle of the room. Grasping the top with one
hand, he pryed the top off. For a moment, Nick stared at the contents. His mind
was rushing a mile a minute. Nick reached for the next crate and opened it. Same
thing. He opened one more. Same thing. Each crate was full of gold.

Nick considered the events of today. Earlier at the mine he observed the Grins
loading a large amount of gold onto the wagon. He surveilled them the entire
trip to this private dock. He watched them enter this vault with the gold and
then he climbed aboard the boat and witnessed the crates full of coal.

Nick scowled at himself for not seeing this sooner. He thought back to the
moment at the base camp when he returned with the sample. He pictured the look
on James’ face when he saw Nick. Nick had seen that look before. He had seen the
look on James years ago when James told Donald that Heather didn’t like him. It
was a look that combined surprise, sadness, and fear.

There was an argument in the tent when Nick arrived. He had heard one of the
Grins talking about San Francisco and then New York. Something about a bank. As
usual, Donald belittled James, but Nick didn’t hear the whole conversation. At
the moment, it didn’t seem important. Donald had said, “There won’t be time in
San Fransisco!” James then mumbled something. “I don’t care about the damn
redwoods, we have to keep moving,” Donald trumpeted back.

All of a sudden Nick understood why the Grins had tried to kill him. This was
much bigger than skimming 10% of the towns money. That seemed forgivable, almost
expected. What was not forgivable was what Nick had stumbled upon. The Grins
didn’t steal 10% of the gold from the mine, the Grins had stolen ALL the gold
from the mine.

Nick looked around the room. There were more than 10 crates. It must be all of
the gold from the last 3 or 4 years.

**((Describe the effects of the annuity and how the town would get no money))**

The day on the mountain, Nick had returned from the climb earlier than expected.
This explained the look of sadness on James. He knew he had to tell Donald that
Nick overheard their conversation. With this much money at stake, the Donald
would likely do anything to keep it safe. James returned to the tent and when
the brothers appeared again, they saw Nick at the fire.

Nick rubbed the bruise on his temple where he was hit by the metal rod. What
happened next he could barely remember, the fight, being thrown off the edge,
the raging river. All for gold.

All for nothing, Nick thought. He was referring to his return to Alaska. He had
returned because he missed this town. He was hoping to move back and start his
life. Now the Grins were about to ruin everything. The town would be broke.
Heather was going to marry Donald. Could people even afford to make the journey
back to Juno? Maybe with the sleigh, Nick could transport people? No, too many
people, that would never work.

**((Possible new chapter about Nick’s dream of moving with Heather to CA. This
would all be ruined))**

What about Heather and her father? What would they do for money?

Nick clinched his fist and slammed the top of the crate of gold. He couldn’t let
this happen. There was still time. He could stop the Grins. He had the sleigh.
He could take the gold.

Suddenly, Nick heard something outside. He went to the entrance, cracked the
door, and peered towards the cabin. It was Duff. He had approached via the cliff
path. He was riding towards the cabin.

Nick scurried around the cave and closed each of the crates then exited the
cave. He returned to the sleigh to see Duff start working on the dock. He was
constructing some sort of large container.

Disgusted, Nick lifted the sleigh off the ground and soared over the mountain.
This was time for a plan. The Grins weren’t going to ruin the lives of the
entire town. Everything was making sense to Nick. Finding this sleigh had not
been a random occurrence. He found this for a reason. He was going to retrieve
the gold. He would returned it to the people. He would save the town.
