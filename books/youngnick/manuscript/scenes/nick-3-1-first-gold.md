**Nick steals the first bit of gold**

Nick had slipped through the front door and found the key.

The floorboards creak loudly. Nick knew the Grins slept in the next building
over, but between the beating of his heart in his ears and the creaking of the
floorboards, he didn’t know how they couldn’t hear him.

**(Describe the room)**

He decided to play it conservative, he took a small nugget from a number of
bags. He then placed a nugget of coal in each bag to give it a relatively equal
weight.

There was just enough moonlight that Nick didn’t need a lamp. He moved as fast
as he could. Nick reached into the safe to grab the second to last bag, but it
was upside down. As he pulled the sack out of the safe, all of the gold spilled.
Half of the take ended up in the safe and the other half on the ground. Nick let
out a short gasp and cursed himself for hurrying.

Just then he heard a noise. Nick froze.

He peered through a crack in the desk and saw a dog approach the door through a
window.

Nick didn’t know they had guard dogs!

Inspite of his predicament, Nick had a momentary feeling of nostalgia. Nick
recognized the dog. It was Old Gus.

Old Gus was a sleepy hound that had been loafing around the mine since Nick was
a kid. Nick and Grin brothers spent hours playing chase and catch with the dog.
On a few occasions, Nick accompanied the Grins on elk hunts. Old Gus always came
too, but he was never much help. He prefered to sniff the bushes and lay in the
sun.

Nick realized this could not be Old Gus. That dog must have died years ago, but
the resemblance was uncanny. This must be an offspring. Nick smiled and
whispered to himself, “Hello, Young Gus.”

If Young Gus came inside he would quickly see Nick. And Nick didn’t want to take
the chance that Young Gus was as lazy as his father.

Nick was crouched behind the desk holding his breath. Hounds had great senses of
smell. Did the dog smell him? Or did it hear Nick drop the gold? Either way,
Nick didn’t move.

About 30 seconds later, the dog moved on. Nick crept to the window and watched
Young Gus meander down the deck to the door of the Grin’s bunk house. Young Gus
went inside. Nick decided it was time to leave.

Nick had one and a half bags of nugget, he estimated two bags would be enough
for everyone to get a little piece. He need about 3 more minutes. He got right
to work and finished filling the second bag.

Nick never wanted to be a crook, but he felt this was different. For once he
felt he was doing something right. He remembered the story of Robin Hood and
Sherwood forest. Yes, they were crooks but they did it for good. Might for
Right.Nick decided not to compare himself to storybook characters until he was
safely away from this mine.

Nick knew the gold wouldn’t really change anything for the town, but he hoped it
would help moral. He had enjoyed flying over the town and carefully putting the
chocolate on everyone's porch, leaving gold wouldn't be much different.
