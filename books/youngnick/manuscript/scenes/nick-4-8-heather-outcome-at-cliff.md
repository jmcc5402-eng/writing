Where could she have run to? The last Nick saw of Heather she had run up
the coast path with four men chasing her.

He turned the sleigh around a grove of trees and saw her. She was standing
at the edge of a cliff. It was a triangle shaped ledge with the point
stretching over the rocky cliffs and raging river below. At the tip of the
ledge was a lone pine tree. Heather stood behind the tree, inches from the
fall off.

Donald Grin and the others surrounded the tree and were menacing towards
Heather. She temporarily held off the attackers by flailing the metal pipe
from behind the tree. Nick guessed it would only be a few more seconds
before the men organized themselves enough to grab Heather. Or worse, push
her off.

Nick dashed the sleigh towards the men. Duff's hat whizzed off his head as
the backdraft of the sleigh caused each man to stumble. Startled by the
flying object that nearly missed their heads, everyone searched the sky to
see what it was.

After buying Heather a few moments of safety, Nick brought the sleigh
below the cliff line to hide from everyone's view.

When Nick saw Donald reaching for Heather, he slowly maneuvered the sleigh
upwards, directly behind Heather. Donald's mouth gaped wide open when he
saw Nick steering the flying sleigh.

Nick yelled out, "it's over Don, I have the gold, you're going to jail."
The sleigh and the caribou were levitating about five feet from the
cliff's edge.

Donald glanced to the rear of the sleigh. Nick could see his eyes widen
and his jaw stiffen. Donald Grin wasn't used to losing. In a fury, Donald
jumped around the tree and grabbed Heather, putting a knife to her throat.

"Put the gold down or she dies," Donald said, his left hand clenching
Heather's hair and his right holding a hunting knife at her throat.

Before Nick could react, Heather elbowed Donald in the stomach and twisted
around the tree. Donald doubled over in pain.

Suddenly, there was an aftershock. The whole ground shook. Donald was
still doubled over but looked up, the tip of cliff he was standing on
started to crack. As the ground he stood on separated from the cliff,
Donald dove and clawed for the bark of the pine tree. His grip held
momentarily until the rock completely broke free of the cliff. The icy
pine tree slipped through Donalds hands and he cascaded down towards the
raging river.

Instinctively, Nick rushed down after Donald, but he was swept away by the
current. When Nick returned to the cliff he saw Heather's legs dangling
off of the pine tree over the cliff's edge. The tree had uprooted and fell
over during the shock. Heather managed to grab the tree, but she couldn't
stay in that position long.

The sleigh ascended at top speed towards the cliff's peak. Nick's eyes
were locked on Heather. He would not let her fall. He saw Heather's mouth
open wide, but he couldn't hear her scream because the sound of the river
below.

Her arms were struggling to maintain a grip. Suddenly, her eyes widened in
terror and she plummeted towards the rocks and deep water.

Nick screamed out Heather's name and adjusted the angle of sleigh. The
caribou screamed straight across the sky towards the falling girl.

Heather's body had rotated horizontal to the ground. For a brief second,
Nick thought they made eye contact and then Heather's body smacked onto
the front skis of the red sleigh. The sleigh was jolted off its
trajectory. Nick lost his footing and his shoulder slammed into the front
of the sleigh.

He heard a thud and quickly got to his feet. Heather had knocked her head
against the sleigh, she appeared unconscious cradled by the two sleigh
skis.

Right then, the lead caribou let out a loud snort. Nick looked up and saw
the cliff dangerously close in front of them with the sleigh moving at top
speed. He jerked the reigns and the animals buck their heads back. The
sleigh shot straight into the air. The bottom of the sleigh rammed into
the hard rock that made the cliff as the sleigh shot vertically. Splinters
of rock sizzled from under the sleigh as it scraped its way up the cliff.
The sleigh was leaning backwards as the trajectory of the cliff steepened,
only the momentum caused by the sheer speed of the sleigh kept the cargo,
and passengers, inside.

Nick gripped the front of the sleigh to stay upright, but Heather's limp
body began to fall. Hold the reigns in one hand, Nick leaped over the
front of the sleigh grabbing Heather by the arm. Her body flung itself off
the front of the sleigh and dangled in the air. Nick clenched her arm with
all his strength.

At the top of the cliff the sleigh slowly leveled out. Nick held onto
Heather and pulled himself up. The caribou knowingly spotted a clearing
and began to land. As the sleigh touched down, Nick picked up Heathers
body and laid her on the bench of the sleigh. She was safe.
