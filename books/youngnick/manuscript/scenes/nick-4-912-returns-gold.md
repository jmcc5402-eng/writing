**Returns gold to townspeople on 'Xmas', people wake up to heather and gold.**

Heather could hear singing. It sounded like people were outside. Her mind felt
cloudy and her head was aching. She slowly opened her eyes. The first thing she
felt was a searing pain in her temple. She felt her head, it was swollen. She
looked around her room and listened. There was singing coming from the street.

She slowly pulled herself to her feet. With every step she took towards the door
her temple pounded. What was going on?

The light blinded her and make her head reek pain as she opened the front door.
The first person she saw was her father. When their eyes met, she thought he was
about to cry. He gently took her by the shoulders and then hugged her.

The whole town was in the street. It reminded of the small festivals they had
when she was a kid. There was a bonfire and Mrs. Hannigan was dancing by
herself. She smelled fresh fish being cooked. This was celebration.

“In a few days we’ll take the ship to Juno, and then a few weeks later, we’ll be
in Frisco,” Jim said softly.

“We?” she replied.

“Yes, Sweet Pea, after that last few days, I think I’m ready to leave Alaska.”

In front of them on the porch, Heather noticed a large sack. She nudged it with
her toe.

“Take a look,” Jim said.

She sat on the porch bench and opened the sack. Her eyes widened and her heart
stopped. “What is...where...whose gold is this” she stammered.

“Thats yours sweety,” Jim said and let out a chuckle. “Turns out the Grins
suckered us all. There was no annuity. There were no bank accounts. They were
storing all the gold in a secret vault at the cliffs. That there is our share.
More than five years worth of digging in that mine. And even more than we
thought. They were skimming off the top too. We just spent the last few hours
going through the statements and divvying up the money. The whole town feels
rich.”

“What happened on the cliff?” Heather asked.

“What happened was the scariest moment of my life. I thought I’d lost you. With
my own eyes I saw you slip off that pine tree. When I made it to the cliffs
edge, there was no sight of you. The guys carried me back to the city dock. We
were gonna get canoes and head upstream to look for you.”

“But the damndest thing happened, everyones calling it a miracle. When we got to
the dock, you were lying there. And you were next to a stack of gold. They just
put me down next to you and I hugged you, while the boys opened each of the
crates. That was about 4 hours ago.”

“What about Nick?” Heather asked.

“Nick who?”

“Nick Johnson. Maybe it was a dream, but I remember seeing him. He had a very
serious look on his face. But I was so happy to see him.”
