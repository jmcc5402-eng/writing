### Variant (i) — main drafted scene

- **SC1 (4):** Caribou stay caribou and no word is said, and the one
  man who clearly sees the sleigh dies — the wink survives, though
  the surrounding henchmen glimpse more than the concept needs.
- **SC2 (4):** The mid-air catch onto the skis and the cliff-scrape
  climb are the braid's payoff *drafted and alive* — but the villain
  strand resolves by geology, not by the braid.
- **SC3 (2):** Nick saves Grace by grip and nerve, not by one
  learnable skill shown on the page — the knot ladder (§7.9's second
  save) has no top rung here.
- **SC4 (3):** The shoulder slam, the rock splinters, and the
  momentum physics are frontier-real, but the convenient aftershock
  is exactly the whimsy-adjacent luck SC4 exists to forbid.
- **Vanish (3):** Donald dies and tells no one, but four-plus
  henchmen watch a flying sleigh and live — survivable only via
  4.7's "nobody believes them" arrest device, which this variant
  leans on without staging.

## Winner: variant (iii), built on variant (i)'s drafted spine

**Rationale.** The three variants aren't really three scenes — (i) is
the only drafted spine, and (ii)/(iii) are endings grafted onto it. As
a *shape*, (iii) wins on every axis that is locked or load-bearing: it
is the only variant that strengthens rather than survives the §7.9b
vanish (the sole witness is the one man on earth nobody would believe,
and who cannot speak without confessing), the only one §12's series
brainstorm actively needs ("Volume 4 needs Donald alive"), and the
only one whose sketch leaves room to build the TODO's rebuilt climax
to spec — Donald's needle, the father wound answered by restraint,
the knot save with a cost, and no aftershock doing the story's work.
Variant (i) supplies the physical scene the winner needs (the catch on
the skis, the cliff-scrape climb) but as written it resolves its
villain by earthquake — the exact luck THREADS ch21 and the no-luck
rule condemn — and holds Grace by grip where canon demands the knot.
Variant (ii) is the weakest on every criterion and the only one that
genuinely threatens the vanish, but it carries the police pressure the
endgame needs anyway. So: adopt (iii) as the ending shape, on (i)'s
drafted catch, with (ii) mined for pressure — which is also the
reading §12 flagged ("decision 9's ending-variant [TK] now leans hard
toward the Grin-survives variant") and this run confirms under scoring.
