## The convergence check (the circulated grief line)

Run before scoring, as instructed — and the premise needs one
correction: not two but **all three** candidates independently
invented the device of Donald's planted line circulating back to him
(A l.112–119; B l.111–114; C l.106–111). Verified against the real
ch4: the townsman line exists on ch4's page — "Sometimes fewer men
come down than go up. It's the country" (manuscript-v2 l.1091) — and
it genuinely is a worn variant of Donald's mined verbatim line
("Sometimes fewer men return to camp than leave"). Three blind
drafters each noticed that canon holds two variant forms of the same
sentence and each bridged them the same way: Donald planted the line;
the town wore it down. **This is convergence-on-canon, not
convergence-on-invention** — the echo target is real, and ch4's own
"the way they tell it" already implies the story arrived from
somewhere. The causal link itself (Donald as the line's author) is
new soft canon in all three drafts: it contradicts nothing, it makes
T21 concrete, and three-for-three blind convergence is strong signal
the author should ratify it — but it must be logged into the bible
§13 ledger on adoption, not carried silently (Finding 4).

## FINDINGS (fix regardless of winner)

1. **A l.168–169 contradicts ch1.** "The man who drilled the tins was
   coming down to make his reading" — ch1 (manuscript-v2 l.58–62)
   says what anybody could read out of six tins was past Nick's
   understanding; Nick collects, he does not read. Who makes the
   reading is TK-1 territory and stays open. Must be fixed in any A
   material used; the point should also be pinned in the next brief
   that touches the sample machinery.
2. **A l.157–164 partially fills TK-1.** "It had gone down the
   Thursday after every expedition for years" and "The bank expected
   it" quietly establish report-to-bank mechanics the brief rules
   uninvented. Strip or bracket on any adoption of A material.
3. **C l.213 breaches the fact manifest.** "The man they had all
   loved died owning a belt" — the belt is not in ch5's manifest, and
   it is gate fact 2's object (keepsake, recognition not
   inheritance). It contradicts nothing (ch1 has the father wearing
   it every day, so Donald plausibly knows it) and the sneer is
   SC1-safe, but manifest-only is the rule: author ratifies or cuts.
4. **The circulation device is new soft canon in all three drafts**
   (see convergence check above): Donald as author of the town's
   "lost his footing / fewer men" story. Contradicts nothing;
   three-way blind convergence argues for ratifying it — but it goes
   into the bible §13 ledger with the adoption PR, and B's loose
   version (B l.113–114 implies the stove man repeated it verbatim)
   should align with ch4's actual worn form, most cleanly by grafting
   A's wording.
5. **A's beat-5 TK placement and age assignment.** The [TK] block (A
   l.209–213) sits directly after the mother's-death sentence — the
   beat's emotional center, adjacent to the banned "[TK] at emotional
   peaks" move (B shows the clean alternative: omit the fact). A also
   assigns the ages (Donald six, James four) where the mined fact
   gives "four and six" unassigned; the assignment is the only
   consistent one, but it is still new — flag on any use.
6. **C l.15 unflagged soft invention.** "His father's fingerless
   gloves" creates a father-keepsake object that echoes the belt's
   recognition motif. Low continuity weight, but it entered no
   handoff flag; de-father the gloves ("fingerless gloves") or
   ratify the detail knowingly.
