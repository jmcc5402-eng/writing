- Bank: "the **Juno Bank**, located in Juno, Alaska" (l.326–327); called

  the last four years" (l.239).
- Discrepancies downstream: "in the five years of the program" (l.1223–1224)
  and Joe's "for the last five years" (l.1980) vs. ch4's four years

  ... in less than 18 months" (l.1194–1202) vs. payout next summer [TK].
- Mechanism matches the bible's "they control the books": the Grins carry
  all mail and the statements (l.1226–1232), print fake invoices (printing

## 6. §7 decision 7 (hybrid compass stone) — contamination map for ch1–4

Decision 7 (locked 2026-07-26): the magic object is the **compass stone,
found by Nick himself** (draft ch23, l.2315–2360); its markings **echo**
the emblems Nick remembers from his father's belt — *recognition, not
inheritance*. **The belt unlocks nothing.** §5's belt-based beats are
explicitly marked for rework and must not leak into the opening chapters.
Verified: the father's belt **does not exist anywhere in the manuscript**
(the only "belt" hits are the verb "belted out," l.312, and James's key on
a "belt loop," l.1644). Ch1–4 as drafted are clean. Every place the old §5
machinery would contaminate them if a drafter "restored" the bible:

1. **Ch1 gear/inventory (l.28–47).** §3 says Nick "Carries: his late
   father's belt ... the key to the sleigh's magic." Do NOT equip Nick
   with a magic belt. If the belt appears at all, it is a plain keepsake —
   no emblems dwelt on, no medallion, no glow, no hint it opens anything.
2. **Ch1 medallion.** §5 step 1 gives the belt "a medallion." Under
   decision 7 there is **no medallion**. Nothing in ch1–4 may plant one.
3. **Ch2 cave discovery (l.214–218).** §4/§5 step 2 describe the cave
   "wall engraved with the same emblems as Nick's belt — the sleigh is
   bound to the belt's lineage." The drafter must NOT engrave
   belt-matching emblems on the cave wall or imply lineage/binding. In the
   manuscript the markings that matter are **on the stone** (a compass,
   l.2336–2339) and the **grooves on the sleigh deck** that receive it
   (l.2351–2360; author's own note at l.583: "((Include the carvings where
   the stones will eventually fit))"). Any ch2 foreshadowing should aim at
   those, not at wall emblems.
4. **Ch2 memory of the father (l.203–206).** Safe zone for decision 7's
   *recognition* half: Nick may remember his father (and, if desired, the
   remembered belt emblems as ordinary memory) — but no suggestion the
   father left him a key to anything.
5. **§1 hook-table leakage.** "The red suit / warm glow — the glow of the
   belt medallion and the sleigh": in ch1–4 no object of Nick's glows,
   ever. The first glow in the book is the buried sleigh's (ch5, l.432+),
   discovered, not inherited.
6. **§5 step 6 warp trigger** ("sets the belt's medallion into the cave's
   engraving"): downstream, but it implies a socket in the cave wall — the
   ch2 cave description must not pre-plant an engraving/socket. The
   manuscript's warp runs through the stone-in-sleigh-groove + North Star
   (l.2351–2392).
7. **§6 BOOK 1 beat "makes camp in a crevice":** the page has Nick camp
   *in the cave itself* (ch5). Minor; don't retrofit a crevice camp into
   ch2 to match the bible.
8. **Framing sentences generally.** Bible language like "the sleigh is
   bound to the belt's lineage" is now anti-canon. The magic answers
   Nick's *choices*; ch1–4 should characterize Nick as the one who earns
   things himself (the climb, the survival skill), which the draft
   already does.
