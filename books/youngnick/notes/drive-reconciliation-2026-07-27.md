## 4. THE BOOK 1 QUESTION

One structural decision blocks everything downstream: **which Book 1 is
the book's Book 1?**

**Lineage A — the trunk (m22 ch1–9), now the v2 chapters.** Longer,
Heather-era, no proposal frame; the reader does not learn Nick's motive
until Donald's Book-3 reveal (the snowflake's design: the v2 ch1–4
deliberately keep "no proposal intent on the page"). This is the lineage
every 2026 decision, plant, and locked ruling was built on.

**Lineage B — the 2015 upload line (yn-1-doc-2015, ch1–7).** Polished to
a submission package, Grace throughout, and it opens on the engine:
"Tonight he would propose to Grace." It also carries, on the page,
things canon wants and m22 lacks: Donald's engagement needle ("Could she
really be engaged to Donald Grin?"), Duff descending to confirm the
kill, the dramatized rock memory — and one thing canon now forbids: the
belt with "A deep red amulet was woven into the buckle" (decision 7
contamination).

**The honest options:**

1. **Stay on A; mine B.** Keep the v2 chapters; lift B's beats one at a
   time as the author rules on them (needle, Duff, rock memory — each
   is already an open Decide item). Cost: the 2015 polish stays
   unused as structure; the reveal stays late. Cheapest; nothing locked
   in 2026 is reopened.
2. **Adopt B's frame into A.** Open the book on the proposal intent and
   rewrite v2 ch1–2 around it. Gain: the romance engine runs from line
   one (the critique's "nobody speaks" problem eases); dramatic irony
   at the dock and the death-news. Cost: reverses the snowflake's
   reveal design (Donald's reveal to Grace keeps its force, but the
   reader-side surprise dies); requires stripping the amulet; ~2
   chapters rewritten plus every downstream plant re-checked.
3. **Adopt B wholesale as Book 1.** Cost: highest — B is MG-register
   (decision 8 violation), belt-magic contaminated, and only reaches
   the sleigh discovery; the v2 chapters are better prose by the
   2026-07-26 review. Listed for completeness, not seriously arguable.

**PROPOSED: option 1, with option 2's frame put to the author as the
one real alternative.** The register ruling, decisions 7/9/10, and the
v2 chapters all sit on lineage A; option 2 is a genuine artistic fork
(early irony vs late reveal), which makes it an author ruling under
house rule 4 — but it should be ruled BEFORE ch5 is drafted, because
the two designs plant different things. Until then, every downstream
Write item in `TODO.md` inherits this dependency.
