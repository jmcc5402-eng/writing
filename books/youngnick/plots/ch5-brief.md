   the machine: gold on one side of the desk, paper on the other.
   CARRIES T01 (the swindle machine). SC4: the cold stays real.
   No paragraph of scene-setting ends in a colon or dash.
2. **James and the flyer.** James drifts to the New York flyer;
   Donald snaps him back to the scale. James laughs at his own
   joke — CARRIES the §13 tell (he laughs at everything; in
   private, the laugh is back — retro-charging its absence the
   morning of the push). PLANTS T14 (the talker: his chatter here
   should make a reader believe he could leak a secret later).
   ADVANCES T02 (the New York dream, aloud).

   looking up. Donald's rehearsed grief line, mined near-verbatim,
   NO surname: the fall was the worst moment of his life — at least
   that's what he keeps repeating around town. ADVANCES T21 (the
   Grins seed the cover; the reader watches the town's grief being
   manufactured). This exchange is the register test on Donald:
   cold, fluent, bored with his own lie.
4. **The Thursday paper (OBLIGATION 1).** The sample report was due
   out on the Thursday mail boat — the reason the push happened
   when it did. Donald turns from the gold to the books and handles
   the Thursday problem [TK: mechanics — what goes to Juno, who
   signs it with Nick dead, what the true reading would have shown].
   The cornered-ness made visible: the fiction has a schedule now,
   and the schedule is weekly, and it never stops needing him.
   PLANTS: the push's motive (retro-pays ch1's timing; pays in full
   at the cove, §7.4). Honor the reveal guards — pressure on the
   page, arithmetic off it.
5. **Donald's formation (OBLIGATION 2).** The counting desk was
   their father's desk; before they counted, they watched him
   count. One childhood-scale moment: how old man Grin treated his
   sons vs how the town loved Nick's father — the beloved
   second-in-command whose boy got the warmth the owner's boys
   never did. Facts available: mother dead when they were four and
   six [TK on use]; a boyhood spent at the mine; "owed something
   more"; the repair-the-cabin scoff. [TK: how old man Grin treated
   his sons — canon says only "unappreciated"; the concrete
   incident is open.] ADVANCES T13: greedy becomes wounded; the
   "unending piece of his soul is missing" made concrete. Keep it
   small — one beat, no essay, no self-pity Donald would notice
   himself indulging.
6. **The Grace wire (OBLIGATION 3).** One live line, Donald to
   James or to himself, establishing his designs on Grace
   (courting/coercing per §3) [TK: exact shape — see Obligation 3].
   PLANTS T10's triangle from the villain side (the dock
   misunderstanding later depends on the town having seen Donald

   it defers per Obligation 3 — the brief does not permit a
   half-staged version.
7. **Duff and the clock.** James relays Duff's word: ready in under
   two weeks; once the cargo is in place, they're on their way.
   HANDS FWD the loose escape clock → the decide-to-run chapter
   (§7.5's hard clock installs there, not here). ADVANCES T02;
   T15 stays within the §3 ruling — Duff is town muscle and
   logistics, never a pusher, and stays offstage.
8. **CLOSE — hope as metal.** Donald weighs the last of it and
   names the town's real asset: the metal, and this town's endless

   — the anti-gift that SC1's later quiet gifts answer). Land the
